# Money & Public Finance in the County-Confederal System

**Core principle**: **No level of government may create money, compel acceptance of its currency, or tax beyond its territorial jurisdiction.** Funding flows _up_ only by explicit, revocable consent of the level below.

---

## 1. The Monetary Base (No Central Bank, No Legal Tender)

| Feature                                                   | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Legal tender laws**                                     | Repealed (31 USC §5103 nullified). No currency has privileged status for debt/tax payment.                                                                                             |
| **Federal Reserve**                                       | Abolished. No lender of last resort. No discount window. No monetary policy.                                                                                                           |
| **US Mint**                                               | **Mint coin only** (gold, silver, copper) per Art III §1. No seigniorage beyond minting cost. No paper, no digital dollar.                                                             |
| **Private mints**                                         | Legal. Compete on purity, weight, reputation. Certified by county assay offices.                                                                                                       |
| **Stablecoins / crypto / foreign currency / local scrip** | All legal for any transaction. No capital gains tax on monetary assets (no income tax).                                                                                                |
| **County tax anticipation notes (TANs)**                  | Counties issue short-term notes (90–180 days) accepted for local tax payment. Circulate as local liquidity. Backed by future LVT receipts. No federal backstop.                        |
| **Clearing**                                              | **Decentralized correspondent network**: State-chartered clearinghouses (former Fed district banks, now member-owned co-ops). Net settlement in coin / TANs / gold. No central ledger. |

**Result**: **Competitive currency regime**. People hold what serves them. Counties accept _any_ asset at market rate for taxes. Gresham's Law reversed: good money drives out bad because _taxes don't privilege one unit_.

## 2. County Level (Primary Fiscal Unit)

### Revenue Sources

| Source                   | Mechanism                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Limit                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Land Value Tax (LVT)** | Annual assessment of _unimproved_ land value. Rate set by county commission (typical 2–5%/yr). Paid in any asset at market rate.                                                                                                                                   | **Cannot tax improvements** (buildings, crops, machinery). Cannot exceed 5% without county referendum.      |
| **Consumption Tax**      | Flat rate (0–10%) on retail sales of goods and services. **Housing excluded** (rent and imputed rent) — housing is taxed via LVT on the land component only, preserving the Georgist rule that improvements are untaxed. Collected by merchants, remitted monthly. | **No exemptions, no progressivity, no deductions**. Rate capped at 10% by county charter.                   |
| **County TANs**          | Short-term notes sold to residents/businesses. Redeemed for tax credit. Circulate as money.                                                                                                                                                                        | **Maturity ≤ 180 days**. Total outstanding ≤ 25% of prior year LVT revenue.                                 |
| **User Fees**            | Water, sewer, trash, road tolls, court fees, recording fees.                                                                                                                                                                                                       | **Must approximate marginal cost**. No cross-subsidy.                                                       |
| **Asset Revenue**        | County-owned utilities, fiber, housing, land leases, mineral rights.                                                                                                                                                                                               | **Operated as independent trusts**. Surplus → citizen dividend or capital fund.                             |
| **Fines / Restitution**  | Civil/criminal penalties.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | **No revenue incentive**: 100% of fines > cost of collection → victim restitution fund or citizen dividend. |

### Spending Authority

| Category                            | Constraint                                                                                       |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Sheriff / courts / jail**         | Mandatory. Funded first.                                                                         |
| **Roads / bridges / water / sewer** | Mandatory maintenance. New capacity → referendum.                                                |
| **Health mutual / pension trust**   | Mandatory contributions (see below).                                                             |
| **Militia / civil defense**         | Mandatory minimum (0.5% LVT). Expansion → referendum.                                            |
| **Schools / libraries / parks**     | Discretionary. Funded by consumption tax surplus.                                                |
| **Citizen dividend**                | **Residual**: After mandatory spending + reserves, surplus LVT distributed per capita quarterly. |

### Hard Limits (County Charter)

1. **No debt beyond TANs**. No bonds, no pensions as liabilities. Pension = funded trust (see below).
2. **Balanced budget annually**. No carryover deficit. Unspent LVT → dividend.
3. **Spending cap**: Total outlays ≤ prior year LVT + consumption tax + user fees. No "emergency" exception.
4. **No grants to private entities** except via competitive bid for county services.
5. **Inter-county transfers forbidden**. No county taxes another county's residents.

## 3. State Level (Coordination + Minimum Standards)

### Revenue Sources

| Source                             | Mechanism                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Limit                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **County Contributions**           | Each county remits **fixed per-capita assessment** to state treasury. Rate set by state legislature, **capped at 15% of county LVT revenue**.                                                                | Increase requires **2/3 of county commissions (population-weighted) to veto**.                                                                                           |
| **State LVT**                      | On state-owned land (trust lands, parks, highways).                                                                                                                                                          | Same rules as county LVT.                                                                                                                                                |
| **Severance Tax**                  | Oil, gas, minerals, timber on state land.                                                                                                                                                                    | Rate set by legislature. Revenue → state sovereign wealth fund.                                                                                                          |
| **Interstate Compact Fees**        | Member states pay dues for compact administration.                                                                                                                                                           | Voluntary. State can withdraw.                                                                                                                                           |
| **State Infrastructure Surcharge** | ≤2% of the LVT base of counties participating in the compact(s) it funds, dedicated solely to compact capital and maintenance. Requires Council 2/3 + unanimous consent of participating county commissions. | Non-participating counties pay nothing. Sunsets in 5 years. No general-fund use. The surcharge is a carved-out share of county-collected LVT, not a separate state levy. |
| **Regional Entity Fees**           | Charter fees and supervisory assessments from state-chartered regional entities.                                                                                                                             | Must cover supervisory cost; no profit.                                                                                                                                  |
| **Minimum-Standard Funding**       | State reimburses certified net new costs (Art II §2.1(3): treasurer certification, sortition audit) or provides the function directly.                                                                       | No reimbursement within 90 days = standard void in that county. Pay-as-you-go: adopted only if the budget act appropriates the cost.                                     |

### Spending Authority

| Category                  | Constraint                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **State Guard**           | Funded by county contributions. No offensive equipment.                                                                                                                                   |
| **Appellate Courts**      | Funded by county contributions.                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Interstate Compacts**   | Dues only. No independent programs.                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Land Title Registry**   | Self-funded by recording fees.                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Sovereign Wealth Fund** | Invests severance tax + land revenue. **Dividends to counties** (per capita). No state spending from principal.                                                                           |
| **Minimum Standards**     | Certified net new costs (Art II §2.1(3)). Pay-as-you-go: adopted only if the budget act appropriates the cost. Shortfall → oldest-first seniority; unfunded standard lapses for the year. |

### Hard Limits

1. **No income tax, no sales tax, no corporate tax, no property tax on private land**.
2. **No borrowing**. No state bonds. No pension liabilities (guard pensions = funded trusts).
3. **No grants to counties** (reverse flow only). No equalization transfers. Minimum-standard reimbursement under Art II §2.1 is a reimbursement, not a grant.
4. **Budget = prior year revenue**. No deficit. Surplus → SWF or county rebate.
5. **No standard in force unless funded** in the current budget (Art II §2.1(3)). Shortfall → oldest-first seniority; the newest unfunded standard lapses for that year.

## 4. Federal Remnant (Vestigial)

### Revenue Sources

| Source                           | Mechanism                                                                                                                               | Limit                                                                                     |
| -------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **State Per-Capita Assessments** | Each state pays fixed per-capita amount to US Treasury. Rate set by Congress, **requires majority-of-counties referendum to increase**. | States pay from their own revenue (county contributions). **No direct federal taxation**. |
| **Mint Seigniorage**             | Difference between face value and production cost of coin.                                                                              | **Capped at cost + 2%**. No profit center.                                                |
| **Diplomatic Fees**              | Foreign embassies pay for credentials, consular services.                                                                               | Trivial.                                                                                  |

### Spending Authority

| Category                      | Amount (est.) | Constraint                                      |
| ----------------------------- | ------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| **Mint Operations**           | ~$30M/yr      | Self-funded by seigniorage.                     |
| **IDCO**                      | ~$5M/yr       | Funded by assessments.                          |
| **Diplomatic Receipt Office** | ~$2M/yr       | Funded by assessments.                          |
| **Congress (90-day session)** | ~$10M/yr      | No staff. Travel only.                          |
| **SCOTUS**                    | ~$5M/yr       | No building (meets in state capitols rotation). |

### Hard Limits

1. **Total federal spending ≤ $60M/yr** (adjusted for gold-price inflation).
2. **No borrowing**. No Treasury debt. No Fed. No "full faith and credit" beyond coin.
3. **No grants, no subsidies, no contracts** except Mint suppliers (paid in coin).
4. **Assessments unpaid = no consequence**. Federal shell starves. Feature.

## 5. Pension / Health / Welfare: The Mutual Aid Layer (Not Tax-Funded)

| System                               | Structure                                                                                               | Funding                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **County Pension Trust**             | Defined-contribution. Assets: county land, utilities, infrastructure equity, local biz loans.           | **Worker + employer contributions** (split 50/50, ~10% wages). Invested locally. Portable across counties. |
| **County Health Mutual**             | Non-profit, member-governed. Negotiates direct with providers. Catastrophic reinsurance via state pool. | **Member premiums** (income-based, capped at 8% wages). No tax subsidy.                                    |
| **County Unemployment / Disability** | Mutual aid fund. 26 weeks @ 60% prior wage.                                                             | **Employer contributions** (1.5% wages). Experience-rated.                                                 |
| **State Guard Pension**              | Funded trust. Assets: state land, severance revenue.                                                    | **State + guard contributions**. No unfunded liability.                                                    |
| **Federal**                          | **None**. No Social Security, Medicare, VA, federal pensions.                                           |

**Key**: These are **not government programs**. They are **county-chartered mutuals** — member-owned, democratically governed, portable, competitive. Counties _charter_ them; they don't _run_ them.

## 6. Inter-Level Financial Firewalls

| Barrier                                      | Enforcement                                                                                                 |
| -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **No federal → state grants**                | Constitutionally forbidden. Federal has no money to grant.                                                  |
| **No state → county grants**                 | State has no independent revenue. Only county contributions. Minimum-standard reimbursement is not a grant. |
| **No county → county transfers**             | Each county sovereign. No equalization.                                                                     |
| **No level may assume another's debt**       | Explicit charter prohibition. Default = bondholders lose.                                                   |
| **No level may mandate another's spending**  | Unfunded mandates void. County veto nullifies.                                                              |
| **No level may regulate another's currency** | Competitive currency = no monetary policy coordination.                                                     |

**Transition Trust exception (the only one):** The Transition Trust (`debt-obligations.md`) may issue **time-bounded instruments (≤10 years — the 10-year Trust notes of `debt-obligations.md` §3.3)** during the transition. It is a wind-down vehicle, not a level of government; its instruments are **not government debt**, and the county assessment funding them **sunsets with the Trust at Year 10**. No perpetual liability (Constitution Art IV §7).

## 7. Crisis Finance (War, Pandemic, Asteroid, Grid Collapse)

| Level              | Mechanism                                                                                                                                       | Trigger                                                      | Limit                                             |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **County**         | **Emergency TANs** (1-year maturity, up to 50% prior LVT). Citizen dividend suspended.                                                          | County commission 2/3 vote + sheriff certification.          | Auto-expires 1 year. Renewal requires referendum. |
| **State**          | **Guard mobilization** funded by emergency county assessments (opt-in per county). State Emergency Reserve (≤2% prior-year budget), pre-funded. | Governor request + majority county commissions consent.      | No state debt. No conscription.                   |
| **Federal**        | **Declaration of War** → special war tax _only on consenting counties_ (see defense-lattice.md).                                                | Congress + county referendum + state opt-in + county opt-in. | 1 year max. No debt. No inflation.                |
| **Regional Co-op** | **Mutual aid assessments** per compact terms.                                                                                                   | Compact board unanimous vote.                                | Voluntary. County can withdraw.                   |

**No "lender of last resort." No money printing. No bailouts.** Entities that overextend default. Creditors lose. System clears.

## 9. The Discipline Mechanisms (What Keeps It Honest)

| Mechanism                 | How It Works                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Tax competition**       | All counties compete on LVT rate, consumption tax, service quality. Residents/businesses vote with feet.                                                                           |
| **Currency competition**  | Bad money (inflating scrip) rejected at tax window. Counties accept at _market rate_ — no peg.                                                                                     |
| **County veto**           | State assessment increases: 2/3 of county commissions (population-weighted) to veto. Other state acts: simple majority within 90 days. Federal acts: simple majority, no deadline. |
| **Sortition audits**      | Randomly selected citizens audit county/state books quarterly. Results public.                                                                                                     |
| **Right of exit**         | Contiguous counties can secede (Art IV §3) by 60% referendum. Federal property bought at assessed value.                                                                           |
| **Sunset everything**     | Every tax, fee, assessment, program expires in 5 years unless re-affirmed by originating body.                                                                                     |
| **No qualified immunity** | Officials personally liable for ultra vires spending. Pays from personal bond.                                                                                                     |

**Sequencing note — state surcharge vs. Transition Trust levy:** The state infrastructure surcharge (≤2% of the LVT base of participating counties) may not take effect before Year 2. During Years 2–5, while the Transition Trust levy is in force, the surcharge is capped at 1%; it rises to 2% only after the Trust levy sunsets at Year 5. The surcharge sunsets 5 years after it reaches 2% (Year 10 at the latest). The two levies do not share a base, but the sequencing keeps the total transition burden visible and bounded.

**Exception — Transition Trust (Years 5–10):** The Transition Trust (`debt-obligations.md`) is **exempt from the Year 5 sunset**. It runs a 5-year tail (Years 5–10) to settle legacy claims (debt, pensions, contracts), then dissolves automatically. Its instruments are ≤10 years; its county assessment sunsets with it. This is the _only_ entity permitted to operate past Year 5, and it has a hard end at Year 10.

### Voluntary Conversion Premium (Equity-Driven Levy)

Per `debt-obligations.md` §3.4: High-income/high-wealth payers may **opt in** to direct their transition levy into **county infrastructure mutuals** (grid, fiber, rail, ports, water) receiving **proportional equity stakes**. Turns punitive levy into productive equity; aligns capital with local infrastructure; voluntary, no coercion.

### Phased Opt-In Debt Swap Program (Retail Holders)

Per `debt-obligations.md` §3.5: Retail Treasury holders (≤$500k/household) may **opt in** to swap Treasuries for **County LVT Credits**, **County TANs**, or **Transition Trust Notes** (par, avoiding haircut). Swap window: Year 0–2. Institutional holders excluded. Anchors domestic liquidity locally; creates immediate demand for county revenue instruments; retail holders avoid haircut.

## 11. Term Limits & Pensions for Officials

**Core principle**: _No special deals. Officials participate in the same mutual-aid systems as everyone else. Power is temporary; accountability is permanent._

### Term Limits

**Note**: County offices (commissioner, sheriff, judge, clerk) are governed by **county charter** (Constitution Art I §3). The limits below are **defaults**; charters may set shorter or longer limits. Federal offices (House, Senate, SCOTUS) are constitutionally fixed (Art III).

| Office                      | Limit                                                                    | Recall Mechanism                                                    |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **County Commissioner**     | 3 terms (4 years each) = 12 years max                                    | County referendum (25% petition → majority vote)                    |
| **County Sheriff**          | 2 terms (4 years each) = 8 years max                                     | County referendum (15% petition → majority vote)                    |
| **County Judge (trial)**    | 12 years max (appointed by commission, confirmed by sortition jury)      | Sortition jury review every 4 years; 2/3 vote removes               |
| **State Legislator**        | 4 terms (2 years each) = 8 years max                                     | District recall (20% petition → majority vote)                      |
| **Governor**                | 2 terms (4 years each) = 8 years max                                     | Statewide recall (15% petition → majority vote)                     |
| **State Attorney General**  | 2 terms (4 years each) = 8 years max                                     | County commission majority recall                                   |
| **State Judge (appellate)** | 12 years max (appointed by legislature, confirmed by sortition jury)     | Sortition jury review every 4 years; 2/3 vote removes               |
| **Federal House Delegate**  | 3 terms (2 years each) = 6 years max                                     | County commission recall (2/3 vote) — already in federal-remnant.md |
| **Federal Senate Delegate** | No term limit — **recallable at will** by state legislature              | State legislature majority vote                                     |
| **SCOTUS Justice**          | 18 years (staggered, sortition-selected) — already in federal-remnant.md | Interposition: 20+ state legislatures nullify rulings               |

**No lifetime appointments. No "safe seats." Every officeholder faces a credible recall threat every election cycle.**

### Pensions for Officials

| Office                                                                | Pension Arrangement                                                                                                                                                          |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **All county officials** (commissioners, sheriff, clerk, judge, etc.) | **Same County Pension Trust** as all residents. Defined-contribution. Worker + employer split (official = worker; county = employer). Portable. No enhanced benefit formula. |
| **All state officials** (legislators, governor, AG, judges, etc.)     | **Same County Pension Trust** (in their home county). No separate state pension system.                                                                                      |
| **Federal officials** (House delegates, Senate delegates, SCOTUS)     | **Same County Pension Trust** (in their home county). No federal pension.                                                                                                    |
| **State Guard officers**                                              | **State Guard Pension Trust** (funded, defined-contribution) — same as enlisted. No separate officer tier.                                                                   |

**Key rules:**

1. **No "golden parachutes."** No lump-sum payouts, no enhanced multipliers, no early vesting.
2. **No dual dipping.** An official who serves at multiple levels (e.g., county commissioner → state legislator) accrues in _one_ trust — their home county's.
3. **Contributions stop when office ends.** No continued accrual after leaving office.
4. **Assets visible.** Trust holdings published quarterly. Sortition audit annually.
5. **No taxpayer guarantee.** Trust is funded, not pay-as-you-go. Market risk borne by participant — same as everyone else.

### Why This Matters

| Problem in Old System                                        | Solution Here                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Congress: 20+ year incumbents, revolving door to lobbying    | 6-year max + recall + no federal pension → citizen-legislators |
| State legislatures: career politicians, gerrymandered safety | 8-year max + district recall + same pension as constituents    |
| Sheriffs: de facto life tenure, qualified immunity           | 8-year max + low-threshold recall + personal liability         |
| Judges: life tenure, unaccountable                           | 12-year max + sortition jury review + interposition            |
| Federal bureaucrats: permanent class, generous pensions      | **No federal bureaucrats exist**                               |

**The incentive structure flips**: Office is a _burden_ you serve for a few years, not a _career_ you build. You return to your community, your trade, your pension trust — same as your neighbors.

## Summary: The Fiscal Constitution

| Level       | Revenue Base                                                                                                                                                     | Spending Limit                         | Debt                             | Currency Role                            |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| **County**  | LVT (land only) + consumption tax + user fees + asset yield                                                                                                      | Prior year revenue. Balanced annually. | **None** (TANs only, short-term) | Accepts all at market rate. Issues TANs. |
| **State**   | County per-capita contributions (≤15% LVT) + state LVT + severance + infrastructure surcharge (carved from county LVT; participating counties only, ≤2%, sunset) | Prior year revenue. No borrowing.      | **None**                         | Accepts all. No issuance.                |
| **Federal** | State per-capita assessments (capped) + mint seigniorage                                                                                                         | **<$60M/yr fixed**. No borrowing.      | **None**                         | **Mints coin only**. No legal tender.    |

**No level can inflate. No level can bail out another. No level can tax beyond its border. No level can compel currency acceptance.**

**Money is a market good. Governance is a subscription service. You pay your county; your county pays your state; your state pays the federal remnant. At any link, you can say "no."**
