# The "United States Military" No Longer Exists

There is **no federal standing army, no Department of Defense, no Joint Chiefs, no unified command structure, no global posture**. What replaces it is a **confederal defense lattice** — layered, voluntary, and accountable downward.

---

## 1. The Nuclear Deterrent (Strategic Layer)

- **Ownership**: Each silo state (MT, ND, WY, CO, NE) holds title to its silos. Submarines → homeport states (WA, GA). Bombers → base states.
- **Launch authority**: **Unanimous consent of all 50 governors** (or their designated councils). **Surviving-majority fallback with proof of life**: Following a confirmed nuclear strike, governors must return a cryptographic proof-of-life ping within a strict time window (default 30 minutes). Governors who fail to respond are presumed unreachable. The denominator shifts from 50 to the number of governors confirmed alive and reachable. A two-thirds majority of those confirmed alive and reachable may authorize launch. The Council maintains a verified-alive registry updated quarterly and a cryptographic proof-of-life protocol tested monthly. No president, no SECDEF, no STRATCOM commander can initiate. _Intentional: any state can veto a launch, so no nuclear retaliation occurs without broad consensus._
- **Maintenance**: Funded by state legislatures. If a state defunds, its silos **stand down (readiness suspended)** — but the warheads remain in place under joint custody. **Physical dismantlement or removal requires consent of 2/3 of states** (or a nuclear compact). A single state cannot unilaterally eliminate the collective deterrent.
- **Doctrine**: **No first use** — constitutional prohibition (Art III §4) + state criminal law. Any officer executing a first-strike order commits a state crime.
- **Result**: A _purely retaliatory_ deterrent. No "escalation dominance," no warfighting nukes. The arsenal shrinks as states choose not to modernize.
- **Design note (the trade-off)**: The deterrent is **intentionally fragile**. No single actor can launch (unanimous consent), and no single state can dismantle it (2/3 for removal). This makes nuclear war nearly impossible to start and impossible to escalate — at the cost of a deterrent that is slow, consensus-bound, and shrinking. That is the point.

## 2. State Guards (Operational Layer)

- **Legal basis**: Title 32 (state active duty) + state constitutions. **No Title 10 federalization** — the federal government _cannot_ call them up.
- **Organization**: 50 separate state military departments. Each governor is commander-in-chief. Adjutants general appointed by governor, confirmed by the Council of Counties.
- **Capabilities**: Determined by each state. Some maintain armor/air (TX, FL, CA). Others: light infantry, cyber, medical, engineers, disaster response.
- **Funding**: State tax revenue + county contributions (see below). No federal dollars.
- **Interoperability**: Voluntary. States adopt common comms/crypto/logistics standards _by compact_ (e.g., "Western States Interoperability Pact"). No mandatory NATO-style STANAGs.

## 3. County Militia / Civil Defense (Tactical / Territorial Layer)

- **Legal basis**: 10 USC §246 (unorganized militia) + state militia acts + county ordinances.
- **Composition**: All able-bodied residents 18–60 (voluntary enrollment for training). No conscription.
- **Training**: County-run, weekend/monthly. Focus: **area denial, infrastructure defense, comms, medical, logistics**. Not offensive maneuver.
- **Equipment**: County-owned: drones, MANPADS, ATGMs, mines, encrypted mesh radios, hardened shelters. Purchased via county LVT surplus or regional co-ops.
- **Command**: Elected county sheriff (or designated militia colonel). No state/federal chain of command.
- **Activation**: Only by **county commission vote** (defense of county) or **governor request + county consent** (state emergency). Exception: the Governor may deploy the Guard for up to 72 hours under Art II §4 coordination emergency; beyond 72 hours, county consent is required.

## 4. Regional Defense Co-ops (Voluntary Aggregation)

- **Formation**: Contiguous counties/states sign mutual aid compacts. Examples emerging:
  - **Great Lakes Compact** (MN, WI, MI, IL, IN, OH, PA, NY)
  - **Western Mountain Compact** (MT, ID, WY, CO, UT, NV, AZ, NM)
  - **Gulf Coast Compact** (TX, LA, MS, AL, FL)
  - **New England Compact** (ME, NH, VT, MA, RI, CT)
- **Functions**: Joint training, shared air defense, cyber threat intel, logistics pooling, standardized munitions.
- **Governance**: Board = one delegate per county commission. Unanimous consent for any offensive operation.
- **No standing HQ**. Rotating secretariat. Dissolvable by any member county at any time.

## 6. Offensive War? Only One Path

1. **Congress passes a Declaration of War** (simple majority, recorded vote, no omnibus).
2. **County referendum**: Majority of _counties nationally_ (weighted by population) must approve within 90 days.
3. **State governors consent**: Each state must opt in its guard units. No state = no troops from that state.
4. **County commissions consent**: Each county must opt in its militia. No county = no troops from that county.
5. **Funding**: Special war tax levied _only_ on consenting counties, collected by county treasurers, paid directly to state/co-op procurement.
6. **Duration**: 1 year max. Renewal requires new referendum.

**No president can start a war. No Congress can fund one without counties. No state can be dragged in. No county can be conscripted.**

## 8. The "FBI / Federal LE" Question

- **FBI abolished**. No federal police.
- **Interstate crimes** (kidnapping, counterfeiting, piracy, treason): Investigated by **state bureaus of investigation** (SBIs), coordinated via **Interstate Compact on Criminal Investigation** — voluntary, county-auditable.
- **Cybercrime / critical infrastructure**: **County/State Cyber Defense Centers** (voluntary). No NSA backdoors.
- **Border**: **County sheriffs on the border** + state guard + county militia. No CBP/ICE. County sponsorship governs entry (see `border-immigration.md`).

> **"To united-states" (v.)** — _to voluntarily coordinate defense across sovereign counties and states for a specific, time-bounded purpose, with unanimous consent at every layer, funded only by those who consent, dissolvable at will._

There is no flag officer who can order a private in Texas to die for a policy made in DC. There is no budget that flows from DC to a base in Germany. There is no "national security" exception to the county veto.

**You wanted to vanish. The military vanished with you.**
