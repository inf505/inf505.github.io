# Agorist / Localist Solutions for Each Taboo Item

**Premise**: You hold dictatorial power _once_. You use it to dismantle central choke points, then vanish. Goal: push authority to the county/municipal level, enable parallel structures, make exit viable.

---

**Tier 1 — essentially untouchable:**

1. **Dollar reserve role / SWIFT** — no mainstream proposal to change it; rarely even discussed.
2. **Nuclear arsenal modernization** — the ~$1.5T plan continues across administrations with almost no debate.
3. **Foreign policy doctrine / global bases** — disputes are over tactics and branding, not the architecture.
4. **Defense budget / military-industrial complex** — real cuts are never on the table.
5. **Surveillance / NSA bulk collection** — periodic reauthorization fights; dismantling is out of bounds.
6. **Intelligence oversight and classification** — almost no mainstream constituency for reform.
7. **Federal Reserve independence / monetary policy architecture** — never structurally debated.
8. **Supreme Court / judicial supremacy structure** — court-packing talked about, not pursued; term limits academic.
9. **Senate malapportionment / filibuster** — constitutionally entrenched (Article V); the 60-vote filibuster treated as immutable.
10. **Corporate constitutional personhood** — _Santa Clara_, _Bellotti_, _Hobby Lobby_; never mainstream-challenged.

**Tier 2 — structural and rarely named:**

11. **Regulatory capture & the revolving door** — industry writes the rules, then hires the regulators.
12. **Agricultural subsidies / food system** —
13. **Tax code structure** (carried interest, step-up basis, mortgage deduction) — campaign rhetoric, little follow-through.
14. **Prison-industrial complex** — cosmetic reform, nothing structural.
15. **Higher education financing** — debt forgiveness debated; the loan model itself is not.
16. **Social Security / Medicare solvency** — the classic third rail.
17. **Civil asset forfeiture** — bipartisan judicial criticism; structural reform stalls.

22. **Israel / Saudi / alliance relationships** — bipartisan at the core; the architecture rarely named as a structural choice.

Each item's county-level solution follows.

## Tier 1: Essentially Untouchable

### 1. Dollar Reserve Role / SWIFT

- **End legal tender laws**: Repeal 31 USC §5103. Allow any currency (gold, crypto, foreign, local scrip) to compete for debts and taxes.
- **Nullify FATCA / CRS reporting** for domestic transactions. Let banks and credit unions opt out of federal surveillance regimes.
- **Charter "free banking" at state level**: States can authorize competitive note issue (pre-1863 model). Counties can clear via bilateral correspondent networks — no Fedwire required.
- **County treasuries accept tax payments in any asset** at market rates. Creates immediate demand for alternatives.

### 2. Nuclear Arsenal Modernization

- **Devolve launch authority**: Require _unanimous_ consent of 50 state governors (or their designated councils) for any offensive nuclear use. No presidential sole authority.
- **Convert ICBM silos to state-owned assets**: Each silo state (MT, ND, WY, CO, NE) holds title. Maintenance funded by state legislatures — if they defund, silos stand down (readiness suspended), but physical removal requires 2/3 of states.
- **Ban first-use by statute** at federal level; make it a state-level crime for any federal officer to execute a first-strike order.
- **County-level civil defense**: Fund fallout shelters, potassium iodide stockpiles, and mesh comms via county tax opt-in.

### 3. Foreign Policy Doctrine / Global Bases

- **Close all overseas bases** not explicitly authorized by a _per-base_ congressional declaration renewed every 2 years.
- **Base host communities (counties) hold veto**: Any foreign base on US soil (or territory) requires annual referendum in the host county.
- **Letters of marque and reprisal restored**: Congress issues specific, time-limited letters for anti-piracy / anti-terror ops. No standing global force.
- **County "sister city" diplomacy**: Counties can form direct trade/cultural pacts with foreign municipalities — bypass State Dept.

### 4. Defense Budget / Military-Industrial Complex

- **Zero-based defense budget**: Every line item expires annually. Re-authorization requires recorded vote in _both_ chambers — no omnibus.
- **Procurement decentralized**: States/guard units procure directly from manufacturers. No federal prime contractors. Counties can band into regional defense co-ops.
- **Convert standing army to state guard cadres**: Active-duty Army shrinks to training/logistics core. Combat power = state guards + county militia (Title 32 + 10 USC §246).
- **Defense contractors lose limited liability** for fraud/defects. Suable in state court by any taxpayer.

### 5. Surveillance / NSA Bulk Collection

- **Abolish FISA Court**. Replace with _county-level warrant requirement_: any electronic surveillance of a US person requires a warrant from a judge in the target's county of residence.
- **Data localization mandate**: All data on US persons stored on US soil, subject to county subpoena only. No federal bulk access.
- **Encrypt-by-default liability**: Telecoms/ISPs liable for damages if they provide unencrypted backhaul to any government agency without a county warrant.
- **Mesh network easements**: Counties can designate public rights-of-way for community-owned encrypted mesh (LoRa, Wi-Fi, fiber). Federal preemption barred.

### 6. Intelligence Oversight and Classification

- **Automatic declassification at 10 years** (not 25). No extensions. Exceptions only for active HUMINT identities — reviewed by a _citizen jury_ in the operative's home county.
- **Classification authority devolved**: Only elected county sheriffs and state governors can classify new material. Federal classifiers lose authority.
- **Whistleblower bounty**: Any citizen who exposes illegal classification gets 10% of the program's budget + immunity. Paid by the agency that over-classified.
- **County "open archives"**: Each county maintains a declassified document repository. Federal agencies must deposit copies.

### 7. Federal Reserve Independence / Monetary Policy Architecture

- **Repeal Federal Reserve Act**. Replace with **decentralized clearinghouse network**: each Federal Reserve Bank becomes a member-owned cooperative chartered by its district's state legislatures.
- **No lender of last resort to insolvent banks**. Discount window closes. States/counties can charter mutual aid clearinghouses for solvent institutions.
- **Treasury issues only coin** (per Art III §1(6)). No bills, no digital dollar. Private mints, stablecoins, local scrip compete.
- **County tax anticipation notes** circulate as local liquidity. Backed by future county tax receipts. No federal backstop.

### 8. Supreme Court / Judicial Supremacy Structure

- **Judicial review limited to _federal_ statutes vs. Constitution**. State law disputes = final in state supreme courts. No SCOTUS certiorari over state law.
- **Court packing via sortition**: 9 justices drawn by lot from a pool of 180 (3 per state, nominated by state legislatures, **confirmed by sortition jury** of citizens). 18-year staggered terms.
- **County jury nullification instruction mandatory**: Judges _must_ inform juries of their right to judge the law. Applies in federal and state courts.
- **Interposition clause**: A coalition of 20+ state legislatures can nullify a SCOTUS ruling for their jurisdictions. Forces constitutional amendment or convention.

### 9. Senate Malapportionment / Filibuster

- **Senate becomes a "Council of States"**: Each state legislature appoints 2 delegates (recallable at will). No popular election. No 6-year terms.
- **Filibuster abolished**; simple majority passes legislation. But: any bill can be _vetoed by a majority of state legislatures_ within 90 days (legislative veto).
- **County "instruction rights"**: County commissions may issue binding instructions to their state's **House delegates** (county delegates). A House delegate who defies the instruction is recallable by the county commission (2/3 vote). Senate delegates remain appointed and recallable by the state legislature (Constitution Art III §2).
- **Revenue bills originate in county assemblies**: Aggregated upward. Federal taxation requires majority of counties nationally (weighted by population).

### 10. Corporate Constitutional Personhood

- **Charter revocation statute**: Any corporation violating criminal law 3x loses its charter. Assets distributed to workers/creditors/community via county court.
- **No Bill of Rights for artificial entities**: Constitutional rights attach only to natural persons. Corporations have only statutory privileges, revocable by the chartering county (or state, for regional entities).
- **County chartering option**: Counties can charter "public benefit corporations" with mandatory worker ownership, profit caps, and dissolution triggers.
- **Liability piercing by default**: Shareholders personally liable for corporate torts/crimes unless corporation carries sufficient insurance (set by county risk boards).

## Tier 2: Structural and Rarely Named

### 11. Regulatory Capture & the Revolving Door

- **Lifetime ban**: Former regulators/legislators barred for life from lobbying or working for regulated entities. Enforced by state AGs, not federal.
- **Regulatory sunset**: Every regulation expires in 5 years unless re-affirmed by _county referendum_ in a majority of affected counties.
- **County "regulatory sandbox"**: Any county can opt out of any federal regulation (except Bill of Rights) by majority vote. Creates 3,000+ policy laboratories.
- **Citizen enforcement**: Any resident can sue to enforce a regulation; 50% of penalties go to plaintiff. No standing barriers.

### 12. Agricultural Subsidies / Food System

- **End all federal commodity subsidies** (corn, soy, wheat, cotton, rice). Cold turkey.
- **County food sovereignty ordinances**: Counties can nullify federal food safety rules for intrastate direct sales (raw milk, on-farm slaughter, cottage foods). Wyoming Food Freedom Act model, nationwide.
- **Land tax shift**: Replace property tax on improvements with **land value tax** (Georgist). Discourages speculative holding; favors smallholders.
- **County grain reserves**: Each county maintains 1-year caloric reserve for its population. Funded by LVT surplus. Breaks just-in-time fragility.

### 13. Tax Code Structure (Carried Interest, Step-Up Basis, Mortgage Deduction)

- **Flat consumption tax at county level only**. No federal income, payroll, capital gains, estate tax. County sets rate (0–10%) on retail sales of goods and services (**housing excluded** — taxed via LVT on land only).
- **Land value tax** (see #12) replaces property tax. No deduction for mortgage interest — no mortgage interest _exists_ in a non-inflationary monetary regime (#1, #7).
- **Carried interest taxed as ordinary income** at county consumption point (when spent). Step-up basis eliminated — basis carries to heir.
- **Tax competition**: Counties compete on rate/service. Residents vote with feet. Federal government funded _only_ by per-capita assessment on states (public-finance.md §4).

### 14. Prison-Industrial Complex

- **Abolish federal criminal code** except: treason, counterfeiting, piracy, crimes on federal enclaves. All else → states/counties.
- **County restorative justice mandate**: For non-violent offenses, county _must_ offer victim-offender mediation, restitution, community service as alternative to cage.
- **Private prisons banned**. All detention facilities county-owned, county-operated. Guards = county employees, elected sheriff accountable.
- **Jury nullification + grand jury review**: Every incarceration reviewed annually by a randomly drawn county grand jury. Can order release.

### 15. Higher Education Financing

- **End federal student loans** (Direct Loan Program, PLUS, guarantees). No bankruptcy exception — dischargeable like any debt.
- **County "apprenticeship charters"**: Counties charter guild-style apprenticeships (tech, trades, care, arts). Employer + apprentice + county = contract. No degree required for licensure.
- **Income-share agreements (ISAs) regulated at county level**: Cap at 10% income for 10 years. Investor = local businesses/alumni, not Wall St.
- **University endowments taxed** as private foundations (5% payout rule) unless they fund county apprenticeship pools.

### 16. Social Security / Medicare Solvency

- **Opt-out at county level**: Any county can vote to exit SS/Medicare. Keeps its payroll tax equivalent; runs its own pension/health mutual aid.
- **County health mutuals**: Non-profit, member-governed. Negotiate directly with providers. No insurance middlemen. Catastrophic reinsurance via state pool.
- **Pension = funded defined-contribution** (not pay-as-you-go). Assets held in county sovereign wealth fund, invested locally (infrastructure, housing, small biz).
- **Intergenerational compact**: Young workers' contributions buy equity in county enterprises (utilities, fiber, housing). Returns fund elders.

### 17. Civil Asset Forfeiture

- **Abolished entirely**. Property seized only _after_ criminal conviction of owner (beyond reasonable doubt). Proceeds → victim restitution fund, not police budget.
- **County "innocent owner" presumption**: Burden on state to prove owner knew/consented. Jury trial mandatory for any seizure >$500.
- **Sheriff personally liable** for wrongful seizure (qualified immunity abolished — see civil-liberties.md §3.3). Pays from personal bond.
- **Community oversight board** (randomly selected county residents) audits every seizure quarterly.

### 18. Forced Arbitration / Class-Action Waivers

- **FAA repealed for adhesion contracts**. No pre-dispute arbitration clause enforceable in consumer/employment/tenant contracts.
- **County small-claims courts expanded**: Jurisdiction for disputes ≤$25k. No lawyers needed. Decided by a resident panel of 3–5 randomly selected residents (like jury duty) — the Art VI §2.4 small-claims jury.
- **Class actions default to _opt-out_ at county level**: Any county resident automatically included unless they opt out. Judgments distributed via county treasurer.
- **Arbitration only by mutual _post-dispute_ agreement**, notarized, with 30-day cooling off.

### 19. Occupational Licensing

- **Universal recognition**: Any license issued by any county valid nationwide. No state barriers.
- **County "right to work" for _all_ occupations**: No license required to offer services. Liability + reputation (bonding, insurance, reviews) replace permission slips.
- **Apprenticeship = license**: Complete a county-chartered apprenticeship (#15) → automatic license. No exam, no fee.
- **Sunset all licenses every 4 years**. County must prove harm from unlicensed practice to renew. Default = freedom.

### 20. Insular Cases / Territorial Colonialism

- **Immediate statehood or independence referendum** for each territory (PR, Guam, USVI, AS, CNMI, etc.). No "commonwealth" limbo.
- **If statehood**: Admitted with 2 Senators, House seats per population. Full Art IV rights.
- **If independence**: Compact of Free Association (COFA) negotiated _county-to-county_ — not federal-to-federal. US counties can form direct aid/trade pacts with new nations.
- **Military bases in territories**: Subject to host county veto (#3). Rent paid to county, not federal treasury.

### 21. House Size / Apportionment

- **Repeal Reapportionment Act of 1929**. House size = cube root of population (~690 today) or Wyoming Rule (1 rep per smallest state's population).
- **Multi-member districts with STV** (single transferable vote) at _county level_. Counties with >1 rep elect proportionally.
- **County "recall + instruct"**: County commission can recall its House member(s) by 2/3 vote. Replacement serves remainder of term.
- **Virtual attendance**: Reps live in their counties, vote remotely. DC becomes ceremonial only. Lobbyists follow reps home — into county sunlight.

### 22. Israel / Saudi / Alliance Relationships

- **No treaty obligation without county ratification**: Any mutual defense pact requires majority vote in a majority of _counties_ nationally.
- **Arms sales = county-licensed exports**: Manufacturers in a county need county commission approval for each foreign sale. Public hearing required.
- **No foreign aid appropriations**. Counties can form "sister region" aid pacts directly (tax-deductible contributions via county treasury).
- **War powers**: Offensive military action requires declaration of war _plus_ majority-of-counties referendum. Defensive only otherwise.

| ------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **County veto**                       | Any federal law/regulation can be nullified by majority vote of county commissions representing >50% of US population. State acts: same majority, within 90 days. |
| **Sortition juries**                  | Randomly selected citizens (stratified by county) replace regulators, grand juries, redistricting commissions, classification review boards.                      |
| **Land value tax**                    | Primary revenue at county level. Unimproved land value assessed annually. Funds county services + citizen dividend.                                               |
| **Right of exit**                     | Any contiguous group of counties can secede to form a new state (Art IV §3) by 60% referendum. Federal property bought at assessed value.                         |
| **Sunset everything**                 | Every federal statute, regulation, agency, treaty, base, program expires in 5 years unless re-authorized by the _county veto_ process.                            |
| **No qualified immunity**             | Government actors personally liable for rights violations. County indemnifies only if actor followed county policy.                                               |
| **Open carry / constitutional carry** | Federal preemption of state/county gun laws repealed. Counties set own rules.                                                                                     |
| **Home rule absolutism**              | Counties exercise all powers not _expressly_ delegated to the Confederation in Art III §1. Presumption of local authority.                                        |

## The "Vanish" Protocol

1. **Enact all above by emergency decree** (your one dictatorial act) — dissolving the old order and promulgating the Constitution for county ratification (provisional until 2,000+ counties approve, target Year 2).
2. **Establish a "Transition Trust"**: Funded by seigniorage from monetary reform (#1, #7) + federal asset sales. Pays for 5-year transition.
3. **Appoint county stewards** (one per county) by sortition from local volunteers. 1-year term. Job: implement, document, train successors.
4. **Dissolve the federal executive branch** except: State Dept (diplomatic receipt only), Treasury (coin mint only), Postal Service (spun to county co-ops).
5. **Publish everything**. Open source the decrees, the code, the data. Walk away.

**Result**: Sovereign counties, loosely confederated for defense and trade. No ruler. No center. Just overlapping circles of voluntary association.
