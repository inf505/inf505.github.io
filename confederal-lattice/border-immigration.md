# Border & Immigration System

**Design Principle**: _Borders are county boundaries. Entry is by county sponsorship. No federal CBP, ICE, USCIS, DHS. Immigration = local labor market + community integration decision._

---

## 1. Constitutional Basis (Art IV, Art VI)

| Provision        | Application                                                                              |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Art I §2**     | County sovereignty = control over who enters/resides in county territory.                |
| **Art IV §2**    | Privileges & immunities = citizens of any county enjoy equal privileges in all counties. |
| **Art IV §3**    | Secession/entry = county referendum (60%). New states = county clusters.                 |
| **Art VI §2.10** | Travel right = move, reside, work across county/state lines. No internal passports.      |
| **Art VI §2.11** | Contract right = freedom to associate, exchange, organize. Sponsorship = contract.       |
| **Art VI §2.13** | Asylum & non-refoulement = **express limit on county entry control** (see §4, §13).      |

**No federal immigration power.** No "plenary power." No "entry fiction." No "expedited removal." No "detention beds."

## 2. County Sponsorship (The Only Path)

### 2.1 Sponsorship = Contract

| Element         | Detail                                                                                                               |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Sponsor**     | County resident (individual), county business, county non-profit, county government, county mutual (health/pension). |
| **Sponsored**   | Any person outside the Confederation seeking entry.                                                                  |
| **Contract**    | Written, notarized, recorded with county clerk. Terms: duration, obligations, support, integration.                  |
| **Duration**    | Initial: 2 years. Renewable indefinitely (county sponsor + sponsored agree).                                         |
| **Termination** | Either party (30 days notice). Sponsored must depart or find new sponsor.                                            |

### 2.2 Sponsor Obligations

| Obligation            | Enforcement                                                                                                                                 |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Financial support** | Sponsor liable for sponsored's public charge (mutual premiums, emergency care, deportation cost). Bond = $10k (individual) / $50k (entity). |
| **Housing**           | Sponsor provides or guarantees housing (county code compliant).                                                                             |
| **Integration**       | Sponsor ensures: language access, cultural orientation, rights education, mutual enrollment, school enrollment (children).                  |
| **Legal compliance**  | Sponsor ensures sponsored complies with county laws. Sponsor not liable for crimes (personal responsibility).                               |
| **Reporting**         | Quarterly check-in (county clerk). Address, employment, mutual status, school.                                                              |

### 2.3 Sponsored Obligations

| Obligation               | Consequence for Breach                                          |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Comply with contract** | Sponsor may terminate.                                          |
| **Obey county laws**     | Criminal = county court. Civil = county court.                  |
| **Enroll in mutual**     | Within 30 days. No gap coverage.                                |
| **Learn English**        | County provides free classes (mutual-funded). Progress tracked. |
| **No public charge**     | Sponsor bond covers. Repeated = sponsorship revocation.         |

### 2.4 County Clerk Role (The "Immigration Office")

- **Records sponsorship contracts** (public, searchable).
- **Issues County Residency Credential** (CRC) — cryptographic ID, valid for sponsorship duration.
- **Tracks sponsorships** (dashboard: active, expired, terminated, violations).
- **Collects sponsorship fees** ($100/yr individual, $500/yr entity — funds clerk + integration).
- **No adjudication**. No "approval." Recording = sponsorship active. Disputes → county court.

## 3. Entry & Exit (Physical Border)

### 3.1 Land Borders (County Sheriff Jurisdiction)

| Border Type                       | Counties                                                                 | Mechanism                                                         |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **International (Mexico/Canada)** | Border counties (TX, NM, AZ, CA; WA, ID, MT, ND, MN, MI, NY, VT, NH, ME) | **County Sheriff = border authority**. No CBP.                    |
| **Ports of Entry**                | County-run (Port Compact).                                               | **County clerk + sheriff + health mutual screener**.              |
| **Between Counties**              | All                                                                      | **Open**. No checkpoints. CRC scanned voluntarily (traffic data). |

### 3.2 Port of Entry Operations (County-Run)

| Function                    | Staff                   | Process                                                                                                         |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Credential verification** | Clerk deputy            | Scan CRC → validate sponsorship contract → entry stamp (digital).                                               |
| **Health screening**        | Mutual nurse            | Symptom check, vaccination record (voluntary), TB test (if high-burden country). No quarantine without warrant. |
| **Security screening**      | Sheriff deputy          | Watchlist check (county + compact databases only). No biometric database.                                       |
| **Customs**                 | Clerk deputy            | Declaration (oral). Duty = county consumption tax (if commercial). No federal tariffs.                          |
| **Asylum claim**            | Clerk + Rights Attorney | **See §4**. Processed at port. No detention.                                                                    |

### 3.3 Air / Sea Entry

| Mode               | Authority                 | Process                                             |
| ------------------ | ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **Commercial air** | County of arrival airport | Clerk/sheriff at gate. CRC scan. Same as land port. |
| **Private air**    | County of landing         | Pilot files manifest. Clerk meets. Same.            |
| **Commercial sea** | Port Compact county       | Clerk/sheriff at dock. Same.                        |
| **Private sea**    | County of landing         | Harbor master notifies clerk. Same.                 |

### 3.4 No-Fly / No-Entry Lists

- **No federal lists recognized**.
- **County watchlist only**: Persons with active felony warrant (county), terrorism conviction (county court), or sponsorship revoked (clerks share via compact).
- **Due process**: Listed person → county court hearing (72 hrs) → removal or clearance.
- **No secret evidence**. No "state secrets privilege."

## 4. Asylum & Protection (County-Level, Rights-Based)

### 4.1 Asylum Claim (At Port or In-County)

| Step                           | Process                                                                                                                   | Timeline    |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| **1. Claim**                   | Person states fear of persecution (race, religion, nationality, political opinion, social group).                         | Immediate   |
| **2. Credible Fear Interview** | County Rights Attorney + interpreter. **Standard: "significant possibility"** (lower than US).                            | 72 hours    |
| **3. If Positive**             | **Parole into county** (CRC issued, sponsorship = county mutual). Mutual covers. Work authorization immediate.            | Same day    |
| **4. Full Hearing**            | County trial court (judge + jury of 7). **No administrative judge.** Government = county DA. Applicant = Rights Attorney. | 180 days    |
| **5. Decision**                | Jury: "Well-founded fear?" Yes = **County Residency Credential (indefinite)**. No = appeal → State Appeals.               | Verdict day |
| **6. Appeal**                  | State Court of Appeals (compact law). Then SCOTUS (if federal statute involved — none).                                   | 90 days     |

### 4.2 Asylum Standards (Broader Than US)

| Ground                   | US Standard                            | County Standard                                                           |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Persecution**          | Government or uncontrolled non-state   | Government OR non-state (county unable/unwilling to protect)              |
| **Gender-based**         | "Particular social group" (litigation) | **Explicit**: FGM, forced marriage, honor violence, reproductive coercion |
| **LGBTQ+**               | "Particular social group"              | **Explicit**: Sexual orientation, gender identity                         |
| **Climate/Disaster**     | Not recognized                         | **Recognized**: County unable to protect (rising seas, drought, conflict) |
| **Generalized Violence** | Not recognized (usually)               | **Recognized**: If county unable to protect (gang war, cartel control)    |

### 4.3 No Detention. Ever.

- **Parole = default**. CRC issued. Mutual enrolls. Sponsor = county mutual (or private if available).
- **Housing**: Mutual contracts with landlords / faith groups / non-profits. County LVT funds emergency shelter.
- **Work**: Immediate authorization (CRC = work permit).
- **Children**: School enrollment immediate. No "public charge" for education.

### 4.4 Resettlement (County Choice)

- **Federal refugee resettlement program = abolished**.
- **County mutuals** opt in to sponsor asylum grantees (per capita allocation via compact).
- **Faith groups / non-profits** co-sponsor (contract with mutual).
- **Integration**: Language, job training, mutual enrollment, cultural orientation (county-funded).

## 5. Immigration Categories (Simplified)

| Category          | Sponsor                                   | Duration                | Path to Indefinite                             |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| **Employment**    | Business                                  | 2 yr, renewable         | 5 yrs continuous sponsorship + mutual standing |
| **Family**        | Resident (spouse, parent, child, sibling) | 2 yr, renewable         | 3 yrs + integration metrics                    |
| **Student**       | School (county-chartered)                 | Program length + 1 yr   | Employment sponsorship after graduation        |
| **Asylum**        | County mutual                             | Indefinite (if granted) | Immediate                                      |
| **Humanitarian**  | County mutual / non-profit                | 2 yr, renewable         | 3 yrs (TPS-equivalent)                         |
| **Investor**      | Business (capital ≥$100k)                 | 2 yr, renewable         | 3 yrs + job creation (5+)                      |
| **Retiree**       | Self (proof of income + health mutual)    | 2 yr, renewable         | 5 yrs                                          |
| **Digital Nomad** | Self (remote income + health mutual)      | 1 yr, renewable         | No (temporary only)                            |

**No "green card." No "citizenship."** County Residency Credential (CRC) = status. Indefinite CRC = permanent residency equivalent. **Naturalization = county citizenship** (see §7).

## 6. Enforcement (No ICE, No Detention Beds)

### 6.1 Status Violations (Civil, Not Criminal)

| Violation                    | Process                                                                | Remedy                                                                      |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Overstay (CRC expired)**   | Clerk notice → 30 days to renew/depart                                 | Departure order (county judge). No arrest.                                  |
| **Sponsorship terminated**   | Sponsor notifies clerk → 60 days to find new sponsor/depart            | Departure order. Sponsor bond covers cost.                                  |
| **Unauthorized entry**       | Sheriff apprehends → clerk screening → asylum claim or departure order | If asylum claim → parole. If not → departure (sheriff transport to border). |
| **Fraud (fake sponsorship)** | DA prosecutes (criminal). Clerk revokes CRC.                           | Conviction = departure order + bar (5 yrs).                                 |

### 6.2 Criminal Aliens (County Court, County Jail)

- **Crime = county court**. No "immigration hold." No "detainer."
- **Conviction** → sentence served in county jail.
- **Post-sentence**: If CRC holder → sponsorship review (sponsor may terminate). If no sponsor → departure order.
- **No mandatory removal for crimes**. Sponsor decides. County judge orders departure if no sponsor.
- **No "aggravated felony" expansion**. County law defines crimes.

### 6.3 Removal (Departure Order)

| Step                  | Mechanism                                                                                  |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **1. Order**          | County judge issues (civil). Sheriff serves.                                               |
| **2. Compliance**     | 30 days voluntary departure. CRC revoked.                                                  |
| **3. Non-compliance** | Sheriff transports to border (land) or airport (air). Cost = sponsor bond / county mutual. |
| **4. Re-entry bar**   | 5 years (fraud), 3 years (overstay), 1 year (sponsorship loss). Waivable by new sponsor.   |

**No detention facilities.** No "expedited removal." No "reinstatement of removal." No "stipulated removal."

## 7. County Citizenship (Naturalization)

### 7.1 Eligibility (After Indefinite CRC)

| Requirement       | Standard                                                                                                         |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Residence**     | 5 years continuous (indefinite CRC).                                                                             |
| **Integration**   | English proficiency (conversational). Civics test (county charter, Constitution Art VI, jury duty).              |
| **Good Standing** | No felony conviction. Mutual current. Taxes current.                                                             |
| **Oath**          | Affirm loyalty to county charter + Constitution Art VI. No "renounce all allegiance" (dual citizenship allowed). |

### 7.2 Process

| Step            | Authority                                                                      |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Application** | County clerk (fee $200).                                                       |
| **Review**      | Clerk verifies requirements.                                                   |
| **Ceremony**    | County commission meeting (public). Oath administered by commission chair.     |
| **Certificate** | County Citizenship Certificate (cryptographic).                                |
| **Rights**      | Vote in county elections. Hold county office. Sponsor without bond. Jury duty. |

### 7.3 Dual Citizenship

- **Permitted**. No renunciation required.
- **County citizenship = political membership**. Other citizenships = other polities' business.
- **Conflict of laws**: County law governs in county. Other citizenship = travel/document only.

## 8. Integration (Mutual Obligation, Not Assimilation)

### 8.1 County Integration Office (Every County)

| Function       | Staff                            | Funding                         |
| -------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| **Language**   | ESL teachers (mutual-funded)     | Mutual assessment (1% premiums) |
| **Civics**     | Rights Attorney + volunteers     | Rights Office budget            |
| **Employment** | Job center (county + mutual)     | Mutual + employer fees          |
| **Housing**    | Landlord liaison (mutual)        | Mutual                          |
| **Health**     | Mutual navigator                 | Mutual                          |
| **Legal**      | Rights Attorney (pro bono panel) | Rights Office                   |
| **Cultural**   | Community groups (grant-funded)  | LVT (0.1%)                      |

### 8.2 Integration Metrics (Tracked, Not Mandated)

| Metric                       | Target            | Consequence if Low          |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------- | --------------------------- |
| **English proficiency**      | 80% at 3 yrs      | More classes (mutual funds) |
| **Employment**               | 75% at 2 yrs      | Job center intensifies      |
| **Mutual enrollment**        | 100% at 30 days   | Clerk follows up            |
| **School enrollment (kids)** | 100% at 30 days   | Truancy = sponsor liability |
| **Civics knowledge**         | 90% pass at 5 yrs | More workshops              |

**No "integration contract" with penalties.** Support, not coercion.

## 9. Interstate Coordination (Compacts)

### 9.1 Sponsorship Recognition Compact (All Counties)

- **CRC issued by any county = valid in all counties** (Art IV §2).
- **Sponsorship contract recorded in any county = enforceable in all counties**.
- **Mutual enrollment in any county = portable** (Art IV §2, public-finance.md).

### 9.2 Asylum Burden-Sharing Compact (Voluntary)

- **Per capita allocation** of asylum grantees (county mutuals opt in).
- **Funding**: State reinsurance pool covers first 2 years. Then mutual standard.
- **Transfer**: Grantee may move to another county (CRC portable). Sending mutual transfers reserves.

### 9.3 Border Security Compact (Border Counties Only)

- **Intelligence sharing**: Sheriff watchlists, trafficking patterns, cartel activity.
- **Joint operations**: Multi-county patrols (mutual aid). No federal involvement.
- **Resource pooling**: Helicopters, sensors, comms (compact-funded).
- **No wall**. Technology + personnel + mutual aid.

### 9.4 International Coordination (No State Dept)

| Need                       | Mechanism                                                                                       |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Readmission agreements** | County-to-county (sister region pacts). Diplomatic Receipt Office facilitates.                  |
| **Visa-free travel**       | County mutual recognition (CRC = visa-free for CRC holders).                                    |
| **Consular access**        | Foreign consulates in county (county permits). No federal role.                                 |
| **Extradition**            | County judge warrant → Governor's warrant → Sheriff transport (Criminal Investigation Compact). |

## 13. Edge Cases & Answers

| Question                                       | Answer                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ---------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Mass migration event (caravan, boat lift)?** | Border sheriffs + mutual aid compact process at ports. Asylum claims heard individually. Parole into sponsoring counties. No camps.                                                                                                                             |
| **County refuses asylum seekers?**             | County mutual opts out of burden-sharing compact. But: if person arrives at port + credible fear → county **must parole** (Art VI §2.13 asylum & non-refoulement right — a constitutional limit on county sovereignty under Art I §2). Sponsor = county mutual. |
| **Sponsor abandons sponsored?**                | Sponsor bond covers costs. Clerk finds new sponsor (mutual, non-profit). Sponsored not penalized.                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Trafficking victim?**                        | Sheriff identifies → Rights Attorney → T-visa equivalent (county humanitarian sponsorship). No prosecution of victim.                                                                                                                                           |
| **Unaccompanied minor?**                       | County child welfare (mutual-funded) = sponsor. Guardian ad litem. Asylum claim if applicable. No ORR. No detention.                                                                                                                                            |
| **Deportation to dangerous country?**          | **Non-refoulement = constitutional** (Art VI §2.13). County judge blocks removal. Asylum claim heard.                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Birthright citizenship?**                    | **County citizenship by birth** (parents = CRC holders). Not mere presence.                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Stateless person?**                          | County mutual sponsors. CRC issued. Path to county citizenship (5 yrs).                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Climate migrant (no persecution)?**          | Humanitarian sponsorship (county mutual). 2 yr CRC, renewable. Path to indefinite (3 yrs).                                                                                                                                                                      |

## 14. The Culture Shift

| Old Mindset                                             | New Mindset                                                           |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Border security = walls, agents, detention"            | **Border security = sponsorship contracts + mutual accountability**   |
| "Immigration = federal plenary power"                   | **Immigration = county contract + community integration**             |
| "Asylum = administrative, detention, expedited removal" | **Asylum = jury trial, parole, mutual support, integration**          |
| "Illegal alien = criminal"                              | **Unauthorized = civil (sponsorship issue). Crime = county court.**   |
| "Deportation = ICE raid, detention, flight"             | **Removal = judge order, 30 days voluntary, sheriff transport**       |
| "Integration = assimilation, English-only"              | **Integration = mutual support, rights education, cultural exchange** |
| "Citizenship = naturalization test, renunciation"       | **County citizenship = oath to charter + Constitution, dual allowed** |
| "Border = federal line"                                 | **Border = county line. Sheriff stands there. Clerk records.**        |

---

**The system is done when**: A person seeking entry finds a sponsor in a county, signs a contract, gets a CRC at the border, enters, enrolls in the mutual, learns the language, works, pays taxes, serves on juries, and after 5 years swears an oath to the county charter — all without a federal agent, a federal court, a federal detention bed, or a federal form. And if the county doesn't want them, the sponsor is liable, not the person. And if the person fears persecution, a jury of county residents hears them. And if the county fails, the compact shares the burden. **Borders are local. Entry is contractual. Rights are absolute. Integration is mutual.**
