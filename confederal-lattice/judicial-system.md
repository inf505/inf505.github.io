# Judicial & Dispute Resolution System

**Design Principle**: _Courts are a service, not a hierarchy. Every dispute resolved at the lowest competent level. No appeal as of right. No supreme court over state/county law._

---

## 1. County Trial Courts (The Workhorse)

### Structure

| Court                         | Jurisdiction                                                            | Selection                                                                                               | Term                                                        |
| ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **General Trial Court**       | All civil >$25,000, all criminal, family, probate, land, contract, tort | Appointed by county commission, **confirmed by sortition jury** (25 residents, 2/3 vote)                | 12 years max. Sortition review every 4 years (2/3 removes). |
| **Small Claims / Mediation**  | Civil ≤$25,000, landlord-tenant, consumer, neighbor disputes            | **Randomly selected residents — panel of 3–5** (like jury duty, 1-year rotation). Trained in mediation. | 1 year. No re-selection for 5 years.                        |
| **Restorative Justice Panel** | Non-violent criminal (theft, fraud, vandalism, drug possession)         | Victim + offender + 3 community volunteers (trained) + facilitator                                      | Ad hoc per case.                                            |

### Procedure

- **No bar membership required** to represent parties. Anyone may advocate.
- **Jury trial** for all criminal + civil >$25,000 (7 jurors, drawn from county voter roll). Small claims (≤$25k) use a resident panel (3–5 residents), which is a jury for Art VI §2.4 purposes.
- **Jury nullification instruction mandatory**: "You may acquit if the law is unjust."
- **Evidence rules**: Minimal. Relevant = admissible. Hearsay allowed (weight to jury).
- **Discovery**: Mandatory automatic disclosure (no games). Sanctions for withholding.
- **Timeline**: Criminal trial within 90 days. Civil within 180 days. Speedy = right, not aspiration.

### Accountability

- **Sortition audit jury** (12 residents) reviews each judge annually: timeliness, fairness, clarity, temperament. Public report.
- **Recall**: 15% petition → majority vote.
- **Personal liability**: Judges personally liable for malicious/knowing rights violations. County indemnifies only for good-faith errors.

### Funding

- **Court fees** (loser pays, capped at 10% judgment).
- **County LVT allocation** (mandatory first priority).
- **No state/federal grants**.

## 2. State Appellate Courts (Error Correction Only)

### Structure

| Court                                    | Jurisdiction                                                                                                                            | Selection                                                                                        | Term                                          |
| ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- |
| **State Court of Appeals** (1 per state) | Appeals from county trial courts on: (a) state compact law, (b) inter-county disputes, (c) constitutional questions under state compact | Appointed by Council of Counties, **confirmed by sortition jury** (3 per county, 2/3 vote)       | 12 years max. Sortition review every 4 years. |
| **Specialized Panels**                   | Water rights, rail, cyber, criminal investigation compact                                                                               | Ad hoc: 3 judges drawn by lot from Appeals Court + 2 subject-matter experts (sortition-selected) | Per case.                                     |

### Procedure

- **Original jurisdiction**: inter-county disputes after compact arbitration fails; cross-county harm claims under Art II §2.1(7); minimum-standard reimbursement-certification disputes under Art II §2.1(3); advisory opinions to the Council of Counties (non-binding).
- **Appeal as of right** for constitutional questions arising under a state compact; discretionary review otherwise. Petition for review granted only if:
  - County courts split on same compact provision, OR
  - Novel constitutional question, OR
  - Inter-county dispute with no precedent.
- **Panel of 3 judges** (drawn by lot). No en banc.
- **Opinion**: Concise. Precedent only for _that compact provision_. No stare decisis across topics.
- **Remand standard**: "Clear error" for facts. "De novo" for compact interpretation.
- **Timeline**: Decision within 120 days of grant.

### Accountability

- Same sortition review + recall as trial judges.
- **Interposition**: 20+ state legislatures can nullify a ruling for their jurisdictions (forces compact renegotiation or constitutional amendment).

### Funding

- **State assessment** (county contributions, capped).
- **Filing fees** (modest, waivable).

## 3. Confederation Supreme Court (Constitutional Boundary Only)

### Structure (Per Constitution Art III §3)

- **9 Justices**: Sortition from 180-person pool (3 per state, nominated by state legislatures, **confirmed by sortition jury** of citizens).
- **18-year staggered terms**. No life tenure.
- **Jurisdiction ONLY**: Confederation statutes vs. this Constitution.
- **No certiorari over state/county law**. State supreme courts = final on state/compact law.

### Procedure

- **Original jurisdiction only**: Cases where a county/state challenges a Confederation statute as ultra vires.
- **Panel of 5** (drawn by lot). Concurrence of 3 required to strike statute.
- **Opinion**: "This statute exceeds Article III §1 delegation." No broad doctrine.
- **No enforcement power**. Relies on voluntary compliance + County Veto + Interposition.

### Accountability

- **Interposition**: 20+ state legislatures nullify ruling for their jurisdictions.
- **Sortition review** every 6 years (citizen jury from all counties).
- **Impeachment**: By Congress (2/3 both chambers) — only for corruption/treason, not rulings.

## 4. Inter-County / Interstate Dispute Resolution

### Mechanism: **Compact Arbitration**

- Each interstate compact includes **dispute resolution clause**:
  1. **Mediation** (30 days) — neutral mediator from compact roster.
  2. **Arbitration** (60 days) — 3 arbitrators: 1 each party, 1 neutral (sortition from compact roster).
  3. **Award** enforceable in county courts of both parties.
- **Arbitration first**; if it fails, the State Court of Appeals has original jurisdiction over the compact-interpretation question.

### County vs. County (No Compact)

- **Direct negotiation** (30 days).
- **County mediation service** (each county maintains roster of trained mediators).
- **Binding arbitration** (mutual consent) → enforceable in either county court.
- **Default**: Status quo ante. No coercion.

## 5. Criminal Justice: Restorative First, Punitive Last

### Triage at Arrest

| Offense Class                                                  | Path                                                                                                            |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Violent felony** (murder, rape, robbery, aggravated assault) | County trial court → jury → prison (county-run, restorative programs mandatory)                                 |
| **Non-violent felony** (burglary, fraud, drug distribution)    | **Restorative Justice Panel** first. If victim agrees + offender completes → no record. If fails → trial court. |
| **Misdemeanor** (DUI, simple assault, petty theft <$1000)      | **Mediation / community service** first. Trial court only if victim demands or repeat.                          |
| **Regulatory / code violation**                                | **Administrative hearing** (county clerk). Fine only. No jail. Appeal → small claims.                           |

### Incarceration (County-Run Only)

- **No private prisons**. County owns/operates.
- **Restorative programs mandatory**: Victim-offender dialogue, restitution, skill-building, therapy.
- **Annual grand jury review**: Randomly drawn county grand jury reviews every incarceration. Can order release.
- **No solitary >15 days**. UN Mandela Rules binding.
- **Voting rights never lost**. Gun rights restored post-sentence (non-violent) or by governor pardon (violent).

### Police / Investigation

- **Sheriff** (elected, 2 terms max, 15% recall) = chief LEO.
- **Deputies**: Hired by sheriff, trained at county academy (6 months: de-escalation, constitutional law, forensics, mental health).
- **No qualified immunity**. Personal liability for rights violations. County indemnifies only if policy followed.
- **Body cams always on**. Footage public (redacted for victims/minors) within 72 hours.
- **Interstate crimes** (kidnapping, counterfeiting, piracy, treason): **State Bureau of Investigation** (SBI) — agents hired by state AG, trained at regional academies. Coordinated via Criminal Investigation Compact. County sheriff retains primary jurisdiction.

## 6. Civil Justice: Access Over Procedure

### Universal Access

- **No filing fees** for claims <$10,000.
- **County legal aid trust** (funded by 1% of court fees + bar donations) provides navigators (non-lawyers) for self-represented.
- **Online filing + virtual hearings** standard. In-person available.
- **Language access**: Interpreters provided free (county cost).

### Specialized Tracks (County-Level)

| Track                   | Scope                                        | Process                                                                |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Family**              | Divorce, custody, support, protection orders | Mediation mandatory first. Judge only if impasse.                      |
| **Land / Housing**      | Eviction, foreclosure, boundary, code        | Expedited (30 days). Mediation first.                                  |
| **Consumer / Contract** | Debt, warranty, service disputes             | Small claims (≤$25k) → resident panel. General (>$25k) → judge + jury. |
| **Tort / Injury**       | Auto, medical, premises, product             | Mandatory mediation (60 days). Then trial.                             |
| **Probate / Estate**    | Wills, trusts, guardianship                  | Administrative (clerk) unless contested.                               |

### Judgment Enforcement

- **Sheriff executes** (wage garnishment, property lien, asset seizure).
- **No judgment-proof evasion**: County tax records + clearinghouse data shared (with warrant).
- **Bankruptcy**: County-administered (Chapter 7/13 equivalent). Discharge in 90 days. Student loans dischargeable. No means test.

## 7. Constitutional Rights Enforcement

### County-Level Remedies

| Right                | Primary Remedy                                  | Forum                                         |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| **Speech/Assembly**  | Injunction + damages                            | County trial court (emergency docket: 24 hrs) |
| **Arms**             | Injunction + damages + attorney fees            | County trial court                            |
| **Due Process**      | Habeas corpus (county judge, 48 hrs)            | County trial court                            |
| **Search/Seizure**   | Suppression + civil damages                     | County trial court                            |
| **Equal Protection** | Injunction + damages + policy change            | County trial court                            |
| **Privacy**          | Injunction + statutory damages ($10k/violation) | County trial court                            |
| **Travel**           | Injunction + damages                            | County trial court                            |
| **Contract**         | Specific performance + damages                  | County trial court                            |

### Structural Remedies

- **County Veto**: Majority of county commissions nullify a federal rights violation anytime; a state rights violation within 90 days.
- **Interposition**: 20+ state legislatures nullify federal rights violation.
- **Secession**: Ultimate exit (Art IV §3).
- **Sortition Audit**: Random citizens investigate systemic violations. Report → county commission → referendum.

## 8. Personnel & Training

### Judges

- **No law degree required**. Citizens with demonstrated judgment (mediators, elders, respected professionals).
- **Training**: 3-month judicial academy (evidence, procedure, constitutional law, restorative justice, bias awareness).
- **Continuing ed**: 20 hrs/year (sortition-selected topics).

### Mediators / Arbitrators

- **County roster**: 50+ trained mediators per county (volunteers, stipend per case).
- **Training**: 40 hrs (mediation ethics, power dynamics, cultural competency).
- **Arbitrators**: Subset with subject-matter expertise (construction, tech, medical, finance).

### Court Staff

- **Clerk** (elected, 4 years): Records, scheduling, filings, warrants.
- **Bailiffs** (sheriff deputies): Courtroom security, jury management.
- **Navigators** (legal aid trust): Assist self-represented.
- **IT / Records**: County employees (open-source case management system).

---

## 9. Technology & Transparency

### Open Courts by Default

- **All proceedings livestreamed** (except juvenile, sealed by judge for compelling reason).
- **All filings public** (redacted for SSN, medical, minors).
- **Real-time docket API** (open source).
- **Judicial analytics dashboard**: Case timelines, outcomes, disparities (public).

### Case Management

- **County-hosted, federated** (each county runs its own instance, interoperable via standard protocol).
- **No central database**. No federal access without county warrant.
- **Blockchain-anchored** filings (tamper-evident, not currency).

## 13. Edge Cases & Answers

| Question                                          | Answer                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Conflict between county charters?**             | Compact arbitration → State Appeals (compact law only).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **County refuses to enforce another's judgment?** | Full Faith & Credit (Art IV §1). Sheriff executes. Refusal = personal liability + County Veto trigger.                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **State compact violates county charter?**        | County charter prevails (Art VII §2). County Veto nullifies compact provision.                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Confederation statute violates rights?**        | SCOTUS strikes (Art III §3). Interposition if SCOTUS refuses.                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Judge corrupt?**                                | Sortition audit → recall referendum → personal liability suit.                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **No lawyer in rural county?**                    | Navigators + resident panels + virtual hearings. No bar monopoly.                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Complex commercial dispute?**                   | Parties choose arbitration (private, enforceable). County court only if arbitration fails.                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Class action?**                                 | **Opt-out at county level** (Art VI §2). County residents automatically included. Judgment distributed by county treasurer.                                                                                                                                                            |
| **State standard lapses mid-case?**               | Savings clause (Art II §2.1(8)): pending claims and prosecutions for pre-lapse conduct continue under the standard as it existed; judgments survive; injunctions survive subject to modification; new post-lapse harms fall to county common law (trespass/nuisance/strict liability). |

---

**The system is done when**: A resident in any county can resolve a dispute — criminal, civil, constitutional — in their own county, in their own language, without a lawyer, in under 180 days, with a jury of their peers, and the judgment is enforced by their sheriff. No higher permission needed.
