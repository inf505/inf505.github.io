# Healthcare Delivery System

**Design Principle**: _Healthcare is a mutual aid obligation, not a market commodity nor a government program. County health mutuals negotiate directly with providers. No insurance middlemen. No federal CMS, FDA, CDC, NIH. Licensing, safety, and standards = county/compact._

---

## 1. County Health Mutual (The Core Institution)

### 1.1 Structure (Per public-finance.md §5)

- **Legal form**: Non-profit, member-governed cooperative (chartered by county).
- **Membership**: All county residents (automatic at birth/residency). Voluntary exit (forfeit mutual benefits).
- **Governance**: **Member assembly** (annual, sortition-selected 50 delegates + 10 at-large). Elects board (9 members, 3-year staggered terms).
- **Board**: 3 clinicians, 3 members, 3 fiduciaries (actuary, accountant, attorney). No provider employees.
- **Executive**: Administrator (hired by board). Medical Director (elected by clinician members).

### 1.2 Financial Model

| Flow                         | Mechanism                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Premiums**                 | Income-based (0–8% of wages), capped at $6k/individual, $12k/family. Collected by employer / self-remitted. |
| **Employer share**           | 50/50 split (wage deduction). Self-employed pay full (tax-deductible).                                      |
| **Catastrophic reinsurance** | State pool (all mutuals contribute 5% premiums). Covers >$100k/episode.                                     |
| **Reserves**                 | 6 months claims + 20% surplus. Invested in county infrastructure (bonds, equity).                           |
| **Surplus distribution**     | Member dividend (premium credit) OR benefit enhancement (member vote).                                      |
| **No profit**                | Ever. No stock. No demutualization.                                                                         |

### 1.3 Benefits (Standard + County Options)

| Category                      | Standard (All Mutuals)             | County Options (Member Vote)                 |
| ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| **Primary care**              | Unlimited, $0 copay                | Extended hours, house calls, telehealth      |
| **Specialty care**            | Referral required (PCP gatekeeper) | Direct access (derm, psych, OB/GYN)          |
| **Hospital**                  | In-network (all county hospitals)  | Out-of-network (emergency only)              |
| **Emergency**                 | $0 copay (anywhere)                | —                                            |
| **Maternity**                 | Full (prenatal, birth, postpartum) | Doula, home birth, birth center              |
| **Mental health**             | 20 sessions/yr + crisis            | Unlimited, residential, psychedelic-assisted |
| **Dental / Vision / Hearing** | Preventive only                    | Full (majority vote adds)                    |
| **Drugs**                     | Formulary (generic first)          | Brand-name, compounding, off-label           |
| **Long-term care**            | 90 days/yr (facility/home)         | Unlimited (majority vote adds)               |
| **Alternative**               | Acupuncture, chiro, PT (20 visits) | Naturopath, ayurvedic, herbalist             |

### 1.4 Provider Payment

| Model                                      | Share | Detail                                                                                 |
| ------------------------------------------ | ----- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Capitation (PCP)**                       | 40%   | $PMPM risk-adjusted. Quality bonuses (HEDIS-adapted).                                  |
| **Episode bundles (specialty/hospital)**   | 35%   | Fixed price per DRG-equivalent. Shared savings.                                        |
| **Fee-for-service (ancillary, emergency)** | 15%   | County fee schedule (120% Medicare 2010).                                              |
| **Value pool**                             | 10%   | Population health metrics (life expectancy, maternal mortality, avoidable admissions). |

**No prior authorization. No claim denials for covered services. Appeal → sortition panel (3 clinicians + 2 members).**

## 2. Provider Licensing & Scope (No State Medical Boards)

### 2.1 County Licensing (Universal Recognition)

- **License = County Charter**: Any clinician licensed in any county practices in all counties (Art IV §2).
- **Requirements**: Graduation from accredited program + national board pass + background check + malpractice bond ($1M).
- **Issuance**: County Clerk (ministerial, 5 days). No board discretion.
- **Renewal**: Biennial. 50 hrs CME (10 hrs ethics, 10 hrs rights, 10 hrs public health). Attestation only.
- **Discipline**: **County Clinical Review Panel** (sortition: 3 clinicians + 2 members). Powers: letter, probation, suspension, revocation. Appeal → State Clinical Appeals Panel.

### 2.2 Scope of Practice (County-Defined, Compact-Harmonized)

| Profession             | Independent Practice                                | Prescriptive Authority           | Supervision Required        |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| **Physician (MD/DO)**  | Full                                                | Full                             | None                        |
| **PA / NP**            | Full (after 2k hrs collaboration)                   | Full (Schedule II+ after 5k hrs) | None post-threshold         |
| **CRNA**               | Full                                                | Full (anesthesia)                | None                        |
| **CNM**                | Full (OB/GYN)                                       | Full (pregnancy-related)         | None                        |
| **Pharmacist**         | Clinical services (MTM, immunizations, POC testing) | Limited (protocols)              | Protocol with physician     |
| **Psychologist (RxP)** | Full (after RxP cert)                               | Psychotropic                     | None post-cert              |
| **Naturopath (ND)**    | Primary care (county option)                        | Limited (formulary)              | None (if county authorizes) |
| **Midwife (CPM/LM)**   | Full (low-risk birth)                               | Limited (obstetric)              | None                        |
| **Acupuncturist**      | Full                                                | Herbs (county formulary)         | None                        |
| **Bodywork / Somatic** | Licensed (county)                                   | None                             | None                        |

**Compact harmonization**: Clinical Practice Compact (voluntary) aligns scopes for telehealth/mobility. Counties opt in.

### 2.3 Malpractice (No Insurance Cartel)

- **County Malpractice Trust** (chartered by county, funded by provider assessments).
- **No-fault first $50k**: Patient files → Trust pays (schedule: injury severity × income). No lawyer needed.
- **>$50k**: Arbitration (3 clinicians, sortition-selected). Binding. Trust pays.
- **Provider assessment**: 0.5% gross revenue (capped at $10k/yr). Experience-rated after 5 yrs.
- **No defensive medicine incentive**: Trust covers all licensed acts within scope.

## 3. Hospital & Facility System

### 3.1 County Hospital (Every County ≥25k Pop)

- **Ownership**: County (via hospital trust — independent, non-profit).
- **Governance**: Board = 3 mutual reps, 3 clinicians, 3 community, 1 county commissioner.
- **Mandate**: Emergency (EMTALA-equivalent), inpatient, surgical, obstetric, ICU, mental health crisis.
- **Funding**: Mutual capitation/bundles + county LVT (capital only) + state pool (trauma/burn/pediatric).
- **No tax-exempt bond advantage**. No 340B. No DSH. No GME subsidies.

### 3.2 Specialty / Regional Hospitals (Compact-Level)

| Type                           | Governance                             | Funding                           | Access             |
| ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| **Trauma Center (Level I/II)** | Trauma Compact (counties in catchment) | Mutual bundles + state pool + LVT | All mutual members |
| **Burn Center**                | Burn Compact                           | Same                              | All mutual members |
| **Pediatric Specialty**        | Pediatric Compact                      | Same                              | All mutual members |
| **Psychiatric Hospital**       | Mental Health Compact                  | Same + county LVT                 | All mutual members |
| **Rehab / LTAC**               | County/Compact                         | Bundles                           | All mutual members |

### 3.3 Ambulatory Surgery / Imaging / Dialysis

- **Ownership**: Provider cooperatives, county, or private (concession).
- **Payment**: Episode bundles (mutual). No facility fees separate from professional.
- **Certificate of Need**: **Abolished**. Market entry = license + mutual contract.

### 3.4 Rural / Frontier Access

- **Critical Access Hospital** (≤25 beds, >35 mi from next): County LVT capital + mutual cost-based reimbursement.
- **Mobile Units**: County-funded (primary care, dental, mammography, lab). Schedule published.
- **Telehealth Hub**: County library/school/fire station = telehealth site (fiber + presenter).
- **Community Health Workers**: County-hired (apprenticeship track). Bridge to mutual.

## 4. Pharmaceuticals & Devices (No FDA)

### 4.1 County Drug Formulary (Every County)

- **Process**: Pharmacy & Therapeutics Committee (clinicians + pharmacists + members, sortition-selected).
- **Criteria**: Evidence (Cochrane, NICE, ICER-adapted), cost-effectiveness, patient preference.
- **Generic first**: Mandatory substitution (patient/clinician can override).
- **Updates**: Quarterly. Public comment. Transparent scoring.
- **Appeal**: Sortition panel (3 clinicians + 2 members) → 30 days.

### 4.2 Drug Approval & Safety (Compact-Level)

| Function                     | Mechanism                                                                                                                       |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **New drug review**          | **Drug Safety Compact** (voluntary, 30+ counties). Joint review of manufacturer dossier + independent analysis (county-funded). |
| **Approval standard**        | **Net benefit > harm** (not just efficacy). Real-world evidence required post-approval.                                         |
| **Conditional approval**     | 2 years. Mandatory registry. Auto-revoked if harm signal.                                                                       |
| **Post-market surveillance** | County adverse event reporting (mandatory, real-time). Compact signal detection (AI + epidemiologists).                         |
| **Recall**                   | Compact issues → County sheriff enforces (seizure, destruction).                                                                |
| **Compounding**              | County-licensed pharmacies. USP <795>/<797>/<800>. Inspected by county.                                                         |
| **Importation**              | County permits (from Drug Safety Compact-approved countries). No federal ban.                                                   |

### 4.3 Pricing (No Patent Monopoly Pricing)

- **Patent (new grants)**: 10 years (not 20). **Compulsory license** after 5 years (royalty = 10% revenue). Existing patents honored at original term (asset-transfer.md §8.1).
- **Price cap**: Median of Drug Safety Compact countries (reference pricing).
- **Direct negotiation**: Mutual negotiates directly with manufacturer. No PBM.
- **Generic entry**: Day 1 post-patent. No "pay-for-delay."
- **Orphan drugs**: Compact funds development (prize model, not monopoly).

### 4.4 Devices & Diagnostics

- **Classification**: County (risk-based: Class I/II/III).
- **Approval**: County (Class I/II) or Compact (Class III). Real-world evidence + post-market registry.
- **Software as Medical Device**: County cyber unit validates (see law-enforcement.md).
- **Right to Repair**: Mandatory. Schematics, parts, diagnostics available to independent techs.

## 5. Public Health & Emergency Response

### 5.1 County Public Health Department (Every County)

- **Structure**: Health Officer (physician, appointed by commission, confirmed by sortition). Staff: epidemiologists, nurses, sanitarians, CHWs, data scientists.
- **Mandate**: Disease surveillance, outbreak response, environmental health, maternal/child health, harm reduction, health equity.
- **Powers**: **No quarantine/isolation without county judge warrant** (due process). No mandatory vaccination. Education + access only.
- **Funding**: 2% LVT (mandatory). Grants from mutual (prevention saves mutual money).

### 5.2 Disease Surveillance (No CDC)

- **County**: Syndromic surveillance (ER, urgent care, pharmacy, school, wastewater). Real-time dashboard.
- **Compact**: Regional epidemiologist pool. Data sharing (anonymized). Outbreak coordination.
- **National**: **No federal reporting**. State compact reporting may be required under Art II §2.1(e). Voluntary compact-to-compact sharing. WHO IHR via Diplomatic Receipt Office (optional).

### 5.3 Outbreak Response

| Phase        | Trigger                           | Action                                                               | Authority                      |
| ------------ | --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| **Monitor**  | Signal (wastewater, syndromic)    | Enhanced surveillance, public alert                                  | Health Officer                 |
| **Contain**  | Cluster (≥5 linked cases)         | Contact tracing (CHWs), testing, isolation (voluntary + warrant)     | Health Officer + Sheriff       |
| **Mitigate** | Community spread (>1% prevalence) | Mask recs (not mandates), ventilation grants, school/work guidance   | Commission (public hearing)    |
| **Surge**    | Hospital capacity >85%            | Mutual activates surge contracts, mobile hospitals, staff mutual aid | Mutual Administrator + Compact |

**No lockdowns. No business closures. No school closures. No mask mandates. No vaccine mandates.** Rights (Art VI) absolute.

### 5.4 Emergency Medical Services (EMS)

- **County EMS** (fire-based or third-service). **No private ambulance monopolies**.
- **Staffing**: Paramedics (county license). Community paramedicine (home visits, mutual-funded).
- **Dispatch**: County 911 (or regional compact). Medical priority dispatch (MPDS).
- **Payment**: Mutual (bundled per response). No balance billing.
- **Air ambulance**: Compact-operated (non-profit). Mutual pays. No surprise bills.

## 6. Mental Health & Substance Use (Integrated, Not Carved Out)

### 6.1 County Behavioral Health Mutual (Sub-Mutual)

- **Same governance** as health mutual. Integrated benefits (no carve-out).
- **Crisis System**: 988 → mobile crisis team (clinician + peer + medic) → crisis stabilization unit (23-hr) → inpatient (if needed) → step-down → outpatient.
- **No involuntary commitment** without: (a) imminent danger, (b) county judge warrant, (c) 72-hr review, (d) jury trial at 14 days.
- **Substance Use**: Harm reduction (syringe service, safe supply, drug checking, OAT) — county choice. No criminal penalties for possession.
- **Peer Workforce**: County apprenticeship (certified peer specialist). Mutual pays.

### 6.2 Psychedelic-Assisted Therapy

- **County option** (member vote). Licensed facilitators (county). MDMA, psilocybin, ketamine.
- **Safety**: REMS-adapted (prep, session, integration). Registry. Compact shares outcomes.

## 7. Long-Term Care & Aging (Mutual, Not Medicaid)

### 7.1 County Long-Term Care Trust (Sub-Mutual)

- **Benefit**: 90 days/yr (facility or home). Member vote → unlimited.
- **Settings**: Home (preferred), adult family home, assisted living, nursing home, co-housing.
- **Workforce**: County apprenticeship (CNA → LPN → RN pathway). Mutual pays tuition + wage.
- **Family Caregiver Stipend**: $1,500/mo (mutual-funded). Respite (30 days/yr).
- **Technology**: Remote monitoring, fall detection, medication dispensing (mutual-funded).

### 7.2 End-of-Life

- **Advance directives**: Standardized, notarized, registry (county clerk). Portable.
- **POLST**: Clinician order. Honored everywhere.
- **Hospice**: Mutual (unlimited). Home, facility, hospital.
- **Medical Aid in Dying**: County option (member vote). Safeguards (2 clinicians, 15-day wait, mental health eval).

---

## 8. Maternal & Child Health (Universal, No Gaps)

### 8.1 Perinatal Mutual (Sub-Mutual)

- **Coverage**: Preconception → 1 yr postpartum. $0 cost-sharing.
- **Providers**: OB, CNM, CPM, doula, lactation, pelvic PT, perinatal psych.
- **Birth Setting**: Hospital, birth center, home (mutual covers all).
- **Postpartum**: 6 visits (2 wk, 6 wk, 3 mo, 6 mo, 9 mo, 12 mo). Home visits (CHW).
- **Newborn**: Automatic mutual enrollment. Well-child (Bright Futures schedule). $0.

### 8.2 Reproductive Rights (Art VI §2.3, §2.11)

- **Abortion**: Legal, covered, no restrictions. Clinician conscience = referral duty.
- **Contraception**: All methods (including sterilization). $0. Pharmacist prescribes.
- **IVF / Fertility**: 3 cycles (mutual). Member vote → unlimited.
- **Gender-Affirming Care**: Covered (WPATH standards). No age gate (clinician judgment).

## 9. Health Data & Technology (Patient-Owned, Federated)

### 9.1 County Health Record (Every Resident)

- **Ownership**: Patient (cryptographic keys). County hosts (utility trust).
- **Architecture**: FHIR + IPFS. Patient grants/revokes access per provider.
- **Interoperability**: Compact health information exchange (HIE) — patient-directed.
- **No national database**. No HIPAA (county privacy law = stricter).

### 9.2 Public Health Data (Anonymized, Real-Time)

- **Source**: Mutual claims + hospital ADT + lab + pharmacy + wastewater + syndromic.
- **Governance**: County Public Health Dept. Compact shares (anonymized).
- **Research**: County IRB (sortition: 3 clinicians, 2 ethicists, 2 members). Patient consent per study.

### 9.3 AI / Decision Support

- **County validates** (cyber unit + clinical review). No black-box mandates.
- **Clinician override** always. Patient informed of AI use.

## 14. Edge Cases & Answers

| Question                                                | Answer                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Resident needs care outside county?**                 | Mutual covers emergency anywhere. Non-emergency: mutual contracts with providers nationwide (compact reciprocity).       |
| **County mutual insolvent?**                            | State reinsurance pool covers. Mutual restructures (premium adjustment, benefit modification — member vote). No bailout. |
| **Provider refuses mutual rate?**                       | Mutual contracts with willing providers. County licenses all — market sorts. No "network adequacy" mandate.              |
| **Drug Safety Compact approves harmful drug?**          | County pulls from formulary. Compact signal detection → recall. Manufacturer liable (strict liability).                  |
| **County refuses public health measure (outbreak)?**    | Health Officer advises. Commission decides. No mandate. Neighboring counties may restrict travel (their right).          |
| **Mental health crisis — police called?**               | **988 → mobile crisis team (no police)**. Police only if weapon + imminent danger. CIT-trained deputies if needed.       |
| **Unlicensed practitioner (herbalist, energy healer)?** | Legal. No license needed. Mutual doesn't cover. Patient chooses. Fraud = county court.                                   |
| **Medical tourism (residents leaving for care)?**       | Mutual covers (emergency + pre-approved elective). County LVT captures return value.                                     |
| **Pandemic vaccine — county mandates?**                 | **No mandate ever**. Mutual covers (free). Public health educates. Rights (Art VI) absolute.                             |

## 15. The Culture Shift

| Old Mindset                                    | New Mindset                                                           |
| ---------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Insurance company decides"                    | **Mutual members decide** (governance, benefits, formulary)           |
| "Doctor orders, patient obeys"                 | **Shared decision-making** (informed consent = right, Art VI §2.4)    |
| "Hospital charges $100k, insurance negotiates" | **Mutual pays bundled price** (transparent, negotiated annually)      |
| "FDA protects us"                              | **County formulary + compact surveillance + strict liability**        |
| "Public health = mandates"                     | **Public health = education, access, trust, wastewater data**         |
| "Mental health = separate, stigmatized"        | **Integrated, crisis-first, peer-supported, rights-respecting**       |
| "Long-term care = Medicaid spend-down"         | **Mutual benefit, home-first, family-supported, tech-enabled**        |
| "Drug prices = market bears"                   | **Reference pricing + compulsory license + direct negotiation**       |
| "Health data = hospital property"              | **Health data = patient property (keys in hand)**                     |
| "Doctor shortage = import more"                | **Grow our own (apprenticeship, service obligation, mutual funding)** |

---

**The system is done when**: A resident in any county, from birth to death, is a member of their health mutual — pays premiums they can afford, sees a clinician they choose, gets drugs at a price their mutual negotiated, gives birth where they want, dies where they choose, and never sees a bill, never fights an insurance company, never fears medical bankruptcy. And if their county mutual fails, the state pool catches them. And if their county public health department fails, the compact epidemiologists help. And if the compact fails, they vote with their feet. **Healthcare is mutual aid. Not a market. Not a government program. Mutual aid.**
