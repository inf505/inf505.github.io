# The Federal Remnant

Almost nothing. The federal government is reduced to a **coordination shell** — no executive branch, no administrative state, no enforcement power, no taxing authority, no police, no military, no intelligence, no regulatory agencies.

---

## 1. Congress (Legislative Only)

| Feature      | Status                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Senate**   | **Council of States**: 2 delegates per state, appointed by state legislature, recallable at will. No 6-year terms. No popular election.                                                                    |
| **House**    | **Cube-root size** (~690 reps). Multi-member districts with STV at county level. Virtual attendance from home counties. Recallable by county commission (2/3 vote).                                        |
| **Powers**   | Only: declare war (requires county referendum), regulate interstate commerce (narrowly), admit states, propose constitutional amendments, receive foreign diplomats. **No general legislative authority.** |
| **Funding**  | **Per-capita assessment on states** (public-finance.md §4). No direct taxation. States pay from their own revenue (LVT, consumption tax).                                                                  |
| **Sessions** | 90 days/year max. No staff. No omnibus bills. Single-subject rule.                                                                                                                                         |

---

## 2. Supreme Court (Judicial Only)

| Feature                | Status                                                                                                                                               |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Jurisdiction**       | **Only**: federal statutes vs. Constitution. **No** cert over state law. State supreme courts = final on state law.                                  |
| **Selection**          | **Sortition**: 9 justices drawn by lot from pool of 180 (3 per state, nominated by state legislatures, **confirmed by sortition jury** of citizens). |
| **Terms**              | 18 years, staggered. No life tenure.                                                                                                                 |
| **Enforcement**        | **None**. Relies on state/county compliance. Interposition: 20+ state legislatures can nullify a ruling for their jurisdictions.                     |
| **Jury nullification** | Mandatory instruction in all federal cases.                                                                                                          |

---

## 3. Executive Branch → **Gone**

| Former Entity           | Replacement                                                                                                                                        |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **President**           | Abolished. No chief executive. No commander-in-chief. No pardon power. No veto (Congress passes laws by simple majority; states/counties nullify). |
| **Cabinet Departments** | All abolished or devolved.                                                                                                                         |
| **White House**         | Museum / event venue.                                                                                                                              |
| **Executive Orders**    | No legal force.                                                                                                                                    |
| **Emergency Powers**    | None.                                                                                                                                              |

## 4. Two Vestigial Offices (Literally 2 Rooms)

| Office                                            | Staff | Function                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ----- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Diplomatic Receipt Office** (former State Dept) | 12    | Accept credentials from foreign ambassadors. Maintain treaty registry. **No outgoing diplomacy** — counties/states/co-ops conduct their own. |
| **United States Mint** (former Treasury)          | 200   | Strike coin (gold, silver, copper) per Art III §1(6). **No paper currency, no digital dollar, no Fed, no debt issuance.**                    |

---

## 5. Two Coordination Nodes (Not Agencies)

| Node                                              | Staff       | Function                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Interstate Defense Coordination Office (IDCO)** | 12          | Maintains secure comms between 50 state adjutants general. Publishes voluntary interoperability standards. **No command authority.** |
| **Governors' Nuclear Council**                    | 0 (virtual) | Encrypted comms link between 50 governors for unanimous launch consent. Meets quarterly. No staff.                                   |

---

## 6. Interstate Compacts (Voluntary, Not Federal)

- **Criminal Investigation Compact** — state bureaus coordinate on kidnapping, counterfeiting, piracy, treason.
- **Cyber Defense Compact** — county/state fusion centers share threat intel.
- **Water / River Compacts** — Colorado River, Great Lakes, etc. (pre-existing, strengthened).
- **Trade / Port Compacts** — regional port authorities, rail corridors.
- **All compacts**: Joined/left by state legislature. County commissions can veto state participation.

---

## 7. Article V Convention (The Only "Reset" Button)

- **Called by 34 state legislatures**.
- **Proposes amendments** ratified by 38 state legislatures (or state conventions).
- **No Congress involvement**. No presidential signature.
- **The only way to change the federal shell**.

## What It _Feels Like_ to Be a Citizen

You **never interact with the federal government**. No IRS. No TSA. No FBI. No Social Security office. No VA. No passport (counties issue travel documents for their residents; foreign countries recognize them or they don't).

You interact with:

- **Your county** (taxes, courts, sheriff, schools, roads, health mutual, militia, zoning, licensing, currency)
- **Your state** (guard, interstate compacts, appellate courts, land title registry)
- **Your regional co-op** (air defense, cyber, water, rail, trade)
- **Your guild / apprenticeship charter** (credentialing, training, insurance)

The federal government is a **myth you read about in history books** — or a 12-person office in Arlington that answers the phone when a foreign ambassador calls.

---

## The One Danger: Drift

The only way this grows back:

1. **Congress claims "implied powers"** under interstate commerce clause → counties must nullify instantly.
2. **States stop paying assessments** → federal shell starves (feature, not bug).
3. **A crisis** (pandemic, asteroid, invasion) → "temporary" federal emergency powers → counties must refuse.
4. **Foreign pressure** — "Who do we talk to?" → "Talk to the relevant counties."

**The system has no immune system but the county veto. Use it or lose it.**
