# Asset Transfer — Federal Lands, Buildings, Infrastructure & IP

**Purpose:** Dispose of everything the old federal state owns — land, buildings, equipment, intellectual property, and critical infrastructure — without creating a successor government.

**Core principle:** _Federal assets are the people's assets. They transfer downward to counties/states, or are sold to fund the debt settlement. Nothing is retained by a central authority._

## 2. Land Transfer Principles

### 2.1 Hierarchy of Disposition

1. **Tribal trust lands** → Recognized tribes (see §6). Non-negotiable.
2. **National Parks / Wilderness** → State conservation trust (no development, no sale).
3. **National Forests** → State trust (timber revenue → SWF; conservation easements).
4. **BLM lands** → State trust (grazing, mining, recreation leases) or county.
5. **DOD lands** → County (base conversion) or state (guard training).
6. **Surplus parcels** → Sold (funds debt settlement).

### 2.2 No Privatization of Parks/Wilderness

- **National Parks, Wilderness Areas, Wild & Scenic Rivers** → State conservation trust.
- **Governance**: State trust board (sortition + conservation experts).
- **Funding**: Entrance fees + state LVT on adjacent land + voluntary donations.
- **No sale, no mining, no logging** (except fire mitigation).

### 2.3 State Trust Lands (BLM/USFS)

- **Ownership**: State (trust).
- **Revenue**: Grazing leases, timber, minerals, recreation → State Sovereign Wealth Fund (public-finance.md §3).
- **Management**: State land board (elected + sortition).
- **Conservation**: 20% set-aside (wildlife, watershed).

### 2.4 Surplus Land Sales

- **Criteria**: Not park, not wilderness, not tribal, not critical habitat, not needed for defense/infrastructure.
- **Process**: Public auction (county). Minimum bid = appraised value.
- **Proceeds**: Transition Trust (debt settlement).
- **Restriction**: Buyer must comply with county zoning + conservation easements.

## 3. Federal Buildings & Facilities

### 3.1 Disposition Matrix

| Type                         | Disposition                                                          |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **DC federal buildings**     | → DC (if statehood) or sold. Transition Trust proceeds.              |
| **Regional offices**         | → County (if used for county services) or sold.                      |
| **Courthouses**              | → County (courts relocate per judicial-system.md).                   |
| **Post offices**             | → County postal co-op (agorist-solutions.md) or sold.                |
| **VA hospitals**             | → County hospital trust (healthcare.md).                             |
| **Military bases**           | → County (base conversion) or state guard.                           |
| **Labs (DOE)**               | → State lab compact (see §5).                                        |
| **Data centers (NSA, etc.)** | → Sold/demolished. Equipment destroyed (no surveillance continuity). |
| **Prisons**                  | → County (law-enforcement.md) or demolished.                         |
| **Embassies**                | → Sold or transferred to host country (no diplomatic corps).         |

### 3.2 Base Conversion

- **Process**: County commission + local redevelopment authority (sortition + experts).
- **Uses**: Housing, industry, parks, schools, hospitals.
- **Environmental**: Cleanup bond from Transition Trust (see debt-obligations.md §12).
- **Timeline**: 5 years to reuse plan; 10 years to full conversion.

## 6. Tribal Trust Lands (56M acres)

### 6.1 Non-Negotiable Transfer

- **All tribal trust lands** → Recognized tribes (sovereign title).
- **No sale, no transfer to states/counties** without tribal consent.
- **Treaty rights** (hunting, fishing, water, sovereignty) honored.

### 6.2 Tribal Sovereignty

| Element             | Arrangement                                                                                                        |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Status**          | Sovereign nations (domestic dependent nations, as under current law) OR counties (if tribe chooses statehood path) |
| **Jurisdiction**    | Tribal courts (criminal/tribal), concurrent with county (major crimes via compact)                                 |
| **Law enforcement** | Tribal police + county sheriff (cross-deputization compact)                                                        |
| **Health**          | Tribal health mutual (IHS → tribal control)                                                                        |
| **Land**            | Trust title (inalienable)                                                                                          |
| **Water**           | Winters doctrine rights honored (compact)                                                                          |

### 6.3 Federal Trust Responsibility

- **Transferred to**: State compact (if tribe agrees) or direct tribal-federal relationship (Confederation Diplomatic Receipt Office — minimal).
- **Funding**: Trust fund (from asset sales) for tribal programs (5-year transition).

## 7. Territories & DC

### 7.1 Puerto Rico (3.2M)

- **Referendum**: Statehood or independence (binding, 2026).
- **If statehood**: Admitted (2 senators, House seats per cube-root).
- **If independence**: COFA-equivalent (county-to-county pacts).
- **Federal assets**: Transferred to new state/nation (bases → county/state guard).

### 7.2 Guam, USVI, American Samoa, CNMI (~350k total)

- **Same process**: Referendum (statehood, independence, or free association).
- **Federal assets**: Transferred.

### 7.3 District of Columbia (670k)

- **Options**: (a) Statehood (2 senators, 1 House), (b) Retrocession to Maryland, (c) Independent city-state.
- **Referendum**: Binding.
- **Federal buildings**: → DC (if statehood) or sold.

## 8. Intellectual Property

### 8.1 Patents (USPTO, 12k employees)

| Element                                | Disposition                                                                                                                                                                 |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Existing patents**                   | Honored at their **original term** (20 years from filing). Vested property right (Art VI §2.12); **not truncated** by the transition.                                       |
| **New patents** (granted after Year 0) | **10-year term**; compulsory license after 5 years (royalty 10%).                                                                                                           |
| **Patent office**                      | → County/compact patent registry (voluntary, mutual recognition).                                                                                                           |
| **Examination**                        | Private examiners (certified) or compact.                                                                                                                                   |
| **Fees**                               | County/compact (no federal).                                                                                                                                                |
| **Transition**                         | Federal patent statute sunsets at Year 5, but **granted patents survive as property** (Art VI §2.12). Registry records existing grants; new grants follow the 10-year rule. |

### 8.2 Copyrights (Copyright Office, LOC)

| Element                                   | Disposition                                                                           |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Existing copyrights**                   | Honored at their **original term** (life + 70). Vested property right; not truncated. |
| **New copyrights** (created after Year 0) | **Life + 50 years** (reduced from 70).                                                |
| **Registration**                          | County/compact registry (voluntary).                                                  |
| **No federal office**                     | Private/compact.                                                                      |

### 8.3 Trademarks

| Element            | Disposition                        |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------- |
| **Existing marks** | Honored.                           |
| **Registration**   | County/compact registry.           |
| **Enforcement**    | County court (Lanham Act-adapted). |

### 8.4 Federal Research IP (Bayh-Dole)

| Element              | Disposition                                                             |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Existing patents** | Honored (university/contractor ownership).                              |
| **Future research**  | County/university/private (no federal funding).                         |
| **Prize model**      | County/compact prizes for public-good research (domestic-policy.md §5). |

## 12. Edge Cases

| Question                                         | Answer                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **State refuses federal land?**                  | County may accept. If neither, land → conservation trust (NGO) or sold.                 |
| **Tribe claims land not in trust?**              | Adjudicated by county court (or compact arbitration).                                   |
| **Buyer of federal land wants to develop park?** | Conservation easement (perpetual) prevents.                                             |
| **Foreign buyer of strategic asset?**            | County/state veto (CFIUS-equivalent at county level).                                   |
| **Nuclear waste site refuses?**                  | Consent-based siting (no federal preemption). Waste stays at reactor (on-site storage). |
| **Lab employees refuse transfer?**               | Severance + pension (debt-obligations.md).                                              |
| **DC residents want statehood?**                 | Referendum → Congress admits (simple majority).                                         |

## 13. The Bottom Line

**~640M acres, 270,000 buildings, 17 national labs, 31 GPS satellites, 600M barrels of oil, 90k tons of nuclear waste, and a $1.7T IP portfolio** — all transferred downward or sold.

**No federal landlord. No federal lab system. No federal infrastructure monopoly. No federal IP registry.**

**The land goes to states and counties. The parks go to conservation trusts. The labs go to university/state consortia. The infrastructure goes to compacts. The surplus goes to the debt settlement. The tribes get their land back.**

**Nothing is retained at the center. The center is empty.**
