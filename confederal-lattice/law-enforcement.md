# Law Enforcement & Investigation System

**Design Principle**: _Policing is a county service. Investigation follows jurisdiction. No federal police. Coordination via compacts, not command._

---

## 1. County Sheriff (Primary Law Enforcement Officer)

### Office

- **Elected**: County-wide, 4-year terms. **2 terms max (8 years)**.
- **Recall**: 15% petition → majority vote (low threshold).
- **Qualifications**: County resident 5+ years. No felony convictions. POST certification (or equivalent).
- **Bond**: Personal surety bond — 10% of county's prior-year LVT revenue, minimum $50k, maximum $500k. Pays judgments for rights violations.

### Authority & Duties

| Duty                              | Scope                                                                                                                                    | Limit                                                                                                                                                         |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Patrol & Response**             | Entire county (unincorporated + municipalities by contract)                                                                              | No jurisdiction outside county without compact — or a court order of competent jurisdiction (duty of office, Art VI §3)                                       |
| **Investigations**                | All crimes originating in county                                                                                                         | Must cede lead to SBI for interstate crimes (Compact Art III)                                                                                                 |
| **Jail Operations**               | County detention facility                                                                                                                | Restorative programs mandatory. Annual grand jury review.                                                                                                     |
| **Court Security**                | Bailiffs, prisoner transport, warrant service                                                                                            | Deputies only. No private contractors.                                                                                                                        |
| **Civil Process**                 | Serve summons, subpoenas, evictions, levies                                                                                              | Fees set by county commission.                                                                                                                                |
| **Emergency Management**          | Coordinate with county civil defense (militia)                                                                                           | No military equipment (no 1033 program).                                                                                                                      |
| **Coroner / Death Investigation** | All unattended/suspicious deaths                                                                                                         | Independent of police. Elected or appointed per charter.                                                                                                      |
| **State Minimum Standards**       | Enforce Art II §2.1 standards within the county                                                                                          | Opt-out does not bar downstream civil action or compact enforcement (Art II §2.1(7)). Cross-county violations → State AG (Art II §6).                         |
| **Cross-County Orders**           | Execute lawful orders from any court of competent jurisdiction — habeas, warrants, injunctions — including orders against another county | Duty of office (Art VI §3, Art II §6). Refusal = personal liability + recall. No occupation. No enforcement of a validly opted-out standard (Art II §2.1(8)). |

### Accountability

| Mechanism                    | Trigger                                   | Consequence                                                                      |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Body Cameras**             | Always on during public contact           | Footage public in 72 hrs (redacted for victims/minors). Tampering = felony.      |
| **Use-of-Force Reporting**   | Every incident (including drawing weapon) | Auto-upload to county dashboard. Annual sortition audit.                         |
| **Civilian Complaint Board** | 11 residents (sortition, 2-year rotation) | Investigates complaints. Recommends discipline. Sheriff must respond in 30 days. |
| **Personal Liability**       | Rights violation (Art VI Constitution)    | Sheriff pays from bond. County indemnifies ONLY if policy followed.              |
| **Recall Referendum**        | 15% petition                              | Majority vote removes. Special election in 60 days.                              |
| **Sortition Audit Jury**     | Annual (12 residents)                     | Reviews policies, training, outcomes, disparities. Public report.                |

### Deputies

- **Hired by Sheriff** (merit-based, background check, psych eval).
- **Training**: 6-month County Academy (see §3).
- **Term**: At-will during probation (1 year). Then **cause-only removal** (Civil Service Board: 3 sortition residents + 2 commissioners).
- **No qualified immunity**. Personal liability for rights violations.
- **Residency**: Must live in county (or adjacent by mutual aid agreement).

## 2. Municipal Police (Optional, Contract-Based)

### Option A: Contract with Sheriff

- Municipality pays county for dedicated deputies.
- Deputies remain county employees. Sheriff commands.
- Municipality sets service level (hours, special units).

### Option B: Municipal Department

- **Chief appointed by mayor, confirmed by city council**.
- **Same standards as deputies**: training, body cams, liability, residency.
- **Jurisdiction**: Municipal boundaries only.
- **Mutual aid**: Automatic with sheriff for felonies, pursuits, disasters.

### Option C: Consolidated

- County charter may merge all municipal LE into sheriff's office.
- Requires majority vote in each affected municipality + county.

## 3. Training: County Academy (Standardized, Not Federalized)

### Curriculum (6 Months / 960 Hours)

| Module                                  | Hours | Focus                                                                                         |
| --------------------------------------- | ----- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Constitutional Law & Rights**         | 120   | Art VI Constitution, search/seizure, due process, equal protection, jury nullification        |
| **De-escalation & Crisis Intervention** | 160   | Verbal judo, mental health, substance use, disability, youth, domestic violence               |
| **Investigative Skills**                | 120   | Interviewing, evidence collection, chain of custody, report writing, digital forensics basics |
| **Firearms & Less-Lethal**              | 120   | Marksmanship, decision-making, taser, pepper spray, baton. **No military weapons**.           |
| **Defensive Tactics**                   | 80    | Control holds, handcuffing, ground defense. No chokeholds.                                    |
| **Community Policing**                  | 80    | Problem-oriented policing, procedural justice, bias awareness, cultural competency            |
| **Legal Procedure**                     | 80    | Warrants, arrests, Miranda, discovery, Brady, courtroom testimony                             |
| **Ethics & Accountability**             | 40    | Duty to intervene, whistleblower protection, personal liability, bond                         |
| **First Aid / Tactical Medicine**       | 80    | TCCC, naloxone, tourniquets, AED, mental health first aid                                     |
| **Technology & Data**                   | 40    | Body cam, RMS, CAD, ALPR policy, cell-site simulator ban, encryption                          |

### Certification

- **County POST Board** (5 members: sheriff, DA, public defender, 2 sortition residents).
- **Recertification**: 40 hrs/year (mandatory: 8 hrs constitutional law, 8 hrs de-escalation, 8 hrs bias).
- **Decertification**: Automatic for felony conviction, rights violation judgment, dishonesty.
- **Portability**: Certification recognized across counties (Art IV §2). No re-academy for lateral transfer.

### Specialized Tracks (Post-Academy)

| Track                         | Duration | Role                                                                                  |
| ----------------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Detective**                 | 8 weeks  | Major crimes, financial crimes, cyber, cold case                                      |
| **K-9**                       | 12 weeks | Detection, tracking, apprehension (bite policy strict)                                |
| **SWAT / Tactical**           | 10 weeks | High-risk warrant, barricade, hostage. **County commission approval per deployment**. |
| **School Resource**           | 6 weeks  | Youth development, not enforcement. No arrest for school discipline.                  |
| **Cyber / Digital Forensics** | 12 weeks | Device extraction, blockchain, OSINT, malware analysis                                |

## 4. State Bureau of Investigation (SBI) — Interstate Crimes Only

### Legal Basis

- **Criminal Investigation Compact** (Art IV Constitution).
- **Member states**: All 50 (voluntary, but practical necessity).
- **Jurisdiction ONLY**: Kidnapping, counterfeiting, piracy, treason, interstate flight, multi-county fraud rings, cybercrime crossing state lines.

### Structure

| Element           | Design                                                                                                            |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Director**      | Appointed by State AG, confirmed by Council of Counties. 4-year term, 1 term max. Recallable by Council majority. |
| **Agents**        | Hired by Director. Same training + 8-week SBI addendum (interstate law, compact procedures, federal liaison).     |
| **Field Offices** | One per major metro region (not per county). Co-located with county sheriff where possible.                       |
| **Lab**           | Regional forensic labs (DNA, digital, ballistics, toxicology). Shared by counties in region.                      |
| **Fusion Center** | Cyber threat intel. **No NSA feed**. County/state sources only. Voluntary participation.                          |

### Authority & Limits

| Power               | Limit                                                              |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Investigate**     | Only Compact-listed crimes. Must notify affected county sheriffs.  |
| **Arrest**          | Only with county sheriff present (or warrant from county judge).   |
| **Subpoena**        | Only for Compact crimes. Enforceable in county court.              |
| **Wiretap**         | County judge warrant (target's county). No FISA. No bulk.          |
| **Asset Seizure**   | **Abolished** (Constitution Art VI §2.5). Conviction required.     |
| **Database Access** | County records only with county warrant. No national DB (no NCIC). |

### Accountability

- **State Inspector General** (elected by Council of Counties, 4 years, 1 term) audits SBI.
- **County Veto**: Any county commission can bar SBI from operating in their county (majority vote).
- **Personal Liability**: Agents + Director personally liable for rights violations.
- **No Qualified Immunity**. Ever.
- **Statewide AG lane**: The Attorney General's narrow statewide prosecution authority (Constitution Art II §6) is separate from the SBI and requires county consent or Council 2/3. It does not create a state police force. Arrests and warrants remain with the county sheriff; if the sheriff refuses, the State Court of Appeals may appoint a special marshal from any qualified sheriff of another county — execution is a duty of office, not compact membership.

## 5. Forensics & Evidence (Decentralized, Accredited)

### Regional Crime Labs

- **One per 5–10 counties** (population ~500k–1M).
- **Governance**: Inter-county compact. Board = 1 commissioner per county.
- **Funding**: Per-capita county contributions. Fee-for-service for non-member counties.
- **Accreditation**: ISO 17025. **County auditors** (sortition) inspect annually.
- **Capabilities**: DNA, latent prints, firearms, toxicology, digital forensics, trace evidence.
- **Turnaround**: 30 days max (violent crimes: 14 days). Backlog >60 days = automatic external review.

### Evidence Management

- **County Evidence Room**: Sheriff responsible. Climate-controlled. RFID tracking.
- **Chain of Custody**: Blockchain-anchored log (tamper-evident).
- **Destruction**: Automatic after statute of limitations + appeals exhausted. Court order required for retention.
- **Brady Database**: County-maintained, shared via compact. Prosecutors certify compliance per case.

### Digital Forensics

- **County Cyber Unit** (sheriff's office): Device imaging, mobile extraction, cloud preservation.
- **Regional Cyber Lab** (SBI): Advanced malware, blockchain, encrypted comms.
- **Standards**: NIST SP 800-86 adapted. Open-source tools preferred (Autopsy, Volatility, Cellebrite alternatives).
- **Encryption**: No mandated backdoors. Compelled decryption = self-incrimination violation (Art VI §2.6).

## 6. Cybercrime & Digital Investigation

### County Cyber Unit (Every County)

- **Staffing**: 2–5 deputies (trained in Cyber Track).
- **Mission**: Ransomware, fraud, CSAM, stalking, doxxing, crypto theft, critical infrastructure.
- **Tools**: Open-source first. County purchases licenses (no federal grants).
- **Partnerships**: Local ISPs, universities, private security firms (contracts, not mandates).

### Regional Cyber Compact (Multi-County)

- **Shared SOC** (Security Operations Center): 24/7 monitoring, threat intel, incident response.
- **Mutual Aid**: Counties assist each other during major incidents.
- **Attribution**: Technical only. **No retaliation authority**. Refer to SBI if interstate.

### Critical Infrastructure Protection

- **County Infrastructure Coordinator** (appointed by commission): Water, power, comms, transport.
- **Vulnerability Disclosure**: Safe harbor for researchers. County runs bug bounty.
- **No Federal CISA**. No mandatory reporting to DC.

## 7. Interstate & International Coordination

### Criminal Investigation Compact (Art IV Constitution)

| Provision               | Detail                                                                                                |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Member States**       | All 50 (opt-out allowed, but loses SBI access)                                                        |
| **Covered Crimes**      | Kidnapping, counterfeiting, piracy, treason, interstate flight, multi-state fraud, cybercrime         |
| **Extradition**         | County judge warrant → Governor's warrant → Sheriff transport. **No federal marshals**.               |
| **Information Sharing** | Compact database (fingerprints, DNA, warrants, stolen property). **County-owned, county-controlled**. |
| **Joint Task Forces**   | Ad hoc. County sheriffs + SBI agents. **County sheriff retains command in their county**.             |
| **Funding**             | Per-capita state assessments. No federal money.                                                       |
| **Governance**          | Compact Council (1 rep per state). Unanimous for new crimes. Majority for ops.                        |
| **Withdrawal**          | 90 days notice. Data returned.                                                                        |

### International (No State Dept, No FBI Legats)

| Need                  | Mechanism                                                                                                               |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Fugitive Abroad**   | County prosecutor → State AG → **Diplomatic Receipt Office** (12 people) → Foreign ministry → Local police. Slow. Rare. |
| **Evidence Abroad**   | Mutual Legal Assistance Treaty (MLAT) — **County-to-county** via sister-region pacts. Or private letters rogatory.      |
| **Cybercrime Abroad** | Private sector cooperation (ISPs, platforms). No government-to-government channel.                                      |
| **Terrorism**         | **No "terrorism" exception**. Treat as crime (murder, conspiracy). County/SBI investigate.                              |
| **Interpol**          | **Not a member**. County sheriffs may join as individuals (private capacity).                                           |

## 9. Intelligence & Surveillance (No NSA, No Fusion Center Feeds)

### County Intelligence Function

- **Sheriff's Intelligence Unit** (2–3 deputies): Open-source (OSINT), local threat assessment, protest monitoring (First Amendment compliant).
- **No covert infiltration** of political/religious groups without county judge warrant + commission approval.
- **No informant payments** without DA approval + commission oversight.

### Regional Threat Assessment

- **Quarterly report**: Crime trends, infrastructure vulnerabilities, natural hazards, civil unrest indicators.
- **Produced by**: SBI analysts + county analysts (shared compact).
- **Classification**: **None**. All reports public (redacted for active investigations).

### Prohibited

- **No bulk collection** (Art VI §2.5).
- **No Stingrays / cell-site simulators** without individual warrant per device.
- **No facial recognition** on public cameras (ban).
- **No ALPR data retention** >30 days (non-hit). Hit data: warrant required for historical query.
- **No predictive policing algorithms** (bias, opacity).
- **No social media monitoring** beyond public posts (OSINT only).

## 11. Personnel System (Professional, Accountable, Local)

### Hiring

- **Background**: Deep (financial, psychological, social media, references). **No polygraph** (unreliable).
- **Disqualifiers**: Felony, domestic violence, hate group membership, prior decertification, dishonesty.
- **Diversity**: Target = county demographics. No quotas. Outreach + mentorship.

### Promotion

- **Merit + Assessment Center** (scenario-based, written, oral). **No seniority-only**.
- **Sergeant → Lieutenant → Captain → Chief Deputy** (max 4 ranks).
- **Sheriff elected** — breaks the "chief" ceiling.

### Discipline

| Level                     | Authority         | Appeal                                        |
| ------------------------- | ----------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| **Counseling**            | Supervisor        | None                                          |
| **Written Reprimand**     | Supervisor        | Civil Service Board                           |
| **Suspension (≤30 days)** | Sheriff           | Civil Service Board                           |
| **Demotion**              | Sheriff           | Civil Service Board                           |
| **Termination**           | Sheriff           | **Civil Service Board (binding arbitration)** |
| **Decertification**       | County POST Board | State POST Board (appeal)                     |

### Wellness

- **Mandatory**: Annual mental health check-in (confidential, no fitness-for-duty unless danger).
- **Peer Support**: Trained deputies (volunteer, confidential).
- **Critical Incident**: Automatic 72-hr leave + peer support + clinician option.
- **PTSD Presumption**: Job-related = covered by county workers' comp (no fight).

## 15. Edge Cases & Answers

| Question                                                 | Answer                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Serial killer crossing counties?**                     | SBI coordinates. County sheriffs retain command in their counties. Joint task force (ad hoc).                                                                                                                 |
| **Cyberattack on power grid (multi-state)?**             | Regional Cyber Compact activates. SBI coordinates if criminal. County Infrastructure Coordinators lead physical response.                                                                                     |
| **Corrupt sheriff?**                                     | Civilian Complaint Board investigates → DA prosecutes → Sortition grand jury indicts → Recall referendum. Personal liability.                                                                                 |
| **County refuses SBI entry?**                            | County Veto (Art III §2). SBI has no authority there. Compact crime goes uninvestigated in that county.                                                                                                       |
| **Sheriff refuses to execute an AG-authorized warrant?** | State Court of Appeals appoints a special marshal from any qualified sheriff of another county — execution is a duty of office, not compact membership. Refusing sheriff faces personal liability and recall. |
| **No sheriff candidate?**                                | County commission appoints interim (sortition-selected residents). Special election in 90 days.                                                                                                               |
| **Tribal police?**                                       | Tribes = counties (if statehood) or independent nations (COFA). Cross-deputization via compact.                                                                                                               |
| **Private security?**                                    | Legal. Licensed by county. **No police powers**. Citizen's arrest only.                                                                                                                                       |
| **Vigilantes?**                                          | Illegal. Sheriff arrests. "Posse comitatus" = only sheriff-summoned, trained, supervised.                                                                                                                     |

| "Intelligence-Led"           | **Community-Led** (OSINT, relationships, trust)             |

---

**The system is done when**: A resident calls 911 (or county equivalent), a deputy arrives in <10 minutes, treats them with respect, resolves the issue or connects them to the right service, and if force is used, the body cam footage is public in 72 hours, the deputy's bond pays if it was wrong, and the sheriff faces recall if it's a pattern. No federal badge overrides the county sheriff. Ever.
